Damage is a marked value that is applied to [[Unit]]s, marked by players and is tracked per-Unit.
The player responsible for the [[Deal]] action that caused the Damage to be marked is the player who marked that Damage. [[Game Effect]]s may refer to that player’s Damage. This means the Damage marked by that player.

When spells, abilities, or other game effects deal damage, Units mark that damage on them temporarily. This can be tracked with coins, dice, or other markers, or by memory.

Damage tracks how close a [[Unit]] is to being [[Kill]]ed. [[Lethal Damage]] is the amount of marked Damage that will cause a unit to die in a [[Cleanup]].

Damage is not a [[Game Object]].

Damage can be [[Heal]]ed. It is automatically done at two specific times:
- At the [[End of Turn]].
- During a [[Combat Cleanup]].
