Some competitions have additional criteria regarding eligibility for 
[[Player|players]]or competition [[Official Role|officials]] (for example, invitation-only competitions).
# [[Player]] Eligibility
Anyone is eligible to participate as a [[Player|player]] in *Riftbound* competitions, unless the [[Player|player]] is:
- Suspended.
- A competition [[Official Role|official]] in that same competition, unless the competition explicitly allows it (non-[[Premier Competition|Premier]] competitions only).
- Specifically prohibited by *Riot* or official partner policy.
- Under 13 years of age and do not have a parent’s or guardian’s permission.
- Prohibited by law, the rules or policy of the [[Competition Organizer]], or by the venue’s rules or policy.
- Restricted by competition addenda regarding age, as long as those restrictions are messaged ahead of time (at low OPL).
# [[Official Role|Competition Official]] Eligibility
Anyone is eligible to participate as a competition [[Official Role|official]], unless the person is:
- Suspended.
- A [[Player|player]] in that same competition, unless the competition explicitly allows it (non-[[Premier Competition|Premier]] competitions only).

If a competition [[Official Role|official]] for an event participates in that event, it must be run at [[Casual OPL]].
Owners of organizations that run [[Premier Competition|Premier]] competitions may not play in those competitions, even if otherwise allowed.
# Riot Employees
Employees of Riot and their families are subject to additional restrictions.
- Family is defined here as any person living with or related to the relevant employee, but not themselves an employee.
- All employees and their family are allowed to participate in local competitions. If the reward is product or cash, the employee or family member may claim that part of the reward.
- Employees must declare themselves to the [[Competition Organizer|CO]].
- The [[Competition Organizer|CO]] may decline to allow Riot employees to participate in the competition for any reason; if so, they must abide by this decision.
- No Riot employee is allowed to play in [[Premier Competition|Premier]] or [[Qualifier Competition|Qualifier]] competitions.
- Families of employees are allowed to play in [[Premier Competition|Premier]] or [[Qualifier Competition|Qualifier]] competitions, if they are otherwise eligible to compete.
