**Derived Information** is a combination of [[Public Information]] and a player’s own skill or calculation. Players are not required to assist opponents in determining **derived information**.

The additional relevant **Derived Information** includes:
- Which public cards have been [[Recycle|Recycled]].
- The order of runes in a [[Rune Deck]].
- Consequences of actions that might happen in the future, such as a [[Unit]] receiving a [[Might Bonus]] from the [[Shield]] ability if a player attacks.
