Complex series of [[Game Action|Game Actions]] are sometimes [[Shortcuts|shortcutted]] into out-of-order sequences, which are technically incorrect or imprecise, but arrive at the correct legal and understood game state.

>*Example:* Drawing a card before channeling runes.

[[Player|Players]] may ask their opponents to do these actions, or a subset of these actions, in the correct order so that they can react at the appropriate time.

Players can’t [[Shortcuts|shortcut]] out-of-order in a way that gives them information prematurely that might affect decisions later in that sequence.

When something is [[Recycle|Recycled]], it must be immediately put on the bottom of the appropriate deck. Failure to [[Recycle]] in this way is a [[Communication Violation]].

>*Example:* A player may not keep their recycled runes facedown on the board to track Energy.
