Cards have a level of [[Privacy]]. These apply to levels of information accessible to the players as well.
In addition to these levels of information, some information is [[Derived Information]].

Like cards, other relevant information about the game state can be [[Public Information]], [[Derived Information]], [[Private Information]], or [[Secret Information]].

The additional relevant [[Public Information]] includes:
- The current [[Turn Player]], phase, step, state of the turn, and who has [[Priority]] and [[Focus]].
- [[Rune Pool|Rune Pools]] and the number and quantity of resources in them.
- All players’ current scores.
- A [[Permanent]]’s current state, as distinct from its printed ones.
- [[Spell|Spells]] and [[Ability|Abilities]] on [[The Chain]], their order, and play choices made for them.
- [[Spell|Spells]] and [[Ability|Abilities]] that resolved previously in the turn, and the sources of them are themselves public.

The additional relevant [[Derived Information]] includes:
- Which public cards have been [[Recycle|Recycled]].
- The order of runes in a [[Rune Deck]].
- Consequences of actions that might happen in the future, such as a [[Unit]] receiving a [[Might Bonus]] from the [[Shield]] ability if a player attacks.

Players must acknowledge changes to [[Public Information]] that result from their cards and effects as those effects would have an observable impact on the game.
As part of acknowledging changes to [[Public Information]], the accountable player must communicate the change to all other players in an understandable and observable way.
>*Examples:*
>- Verbally announcing the change
>- Physically indicating the change
>- Other means of communication understood and agreed upon by all players in the game.

[[Player|Players]] must answer honestly any questions regarding [[Public Information]].
[[Player|Players]] must not represent [[Public Information]] or [[Derived Information]] [[Player|Players]].
[[Player|Players]] must point out discrepancies between announced and recorded [[Public Information]].

[[Judge Role|Judges]] are encouraged to help players determine public information, but not derived information.

In [[2v2 (Magma Chamber)]], teammates may share [[Private Information]], including directly showing each other the cards in their hand.
