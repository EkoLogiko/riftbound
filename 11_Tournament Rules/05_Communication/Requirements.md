[[Player|Players]] are required to communicate honestly about the state of the game with their opponents, and are required to communicate honestly and completely with [[Official Role|Tournament Officials]].

Players are not required to assist their opponents in playing the game.

Players are required to treat other [[Player|Players]], [[Official Role|Tournament Officials]], and [[Spectator|Spectators]] with respect.

In [[Team]] events, communication between teammates is allowed and encouraged, but it must be done in a reasonably timely manner. (see [[Slow Play]])
