At any time before a [[Game]] is completed, any player may [[Conceding|Concede]] that game or all players may mutually agree to draw it.
At any time before a [[Match]] is completed, any player may [[Conceding|Concede]] that match or all players may mutually agree to draw it.

Players can’t agree to concessions or draws in exchange for anything. (see [[Bribery]])

Players can’t scout other [[Match]] results before deciding to [[Conceding|Concede]] or draw their match and need to remain in their seats while their match is in progress.

If a [[Player|player]] refuses to play, tournament [[Official Role|officials]] should treat that as [[Conceding]] the [[Match]].
