Some competitions at [[High OPL]] use video capture of a [[Match]].
Certain events or portions of events may require [[Player|Players]] to appear on camera.

[[Spectator|Spectators]] and [[Player|Players]] who are on camera during matches are responsible for behaving respectfully toward all participants.

[[Spectator|Spectators]] are permitted to record matches using video capture, provided they do so without interfering with the match.

At [[Professional OPL]], the [[Head Judge]] may consult video capture to help make rulings during a match.

[[Judge Role|Judges]] should **never** honor a [[Player]]'s request to consult video capture.
