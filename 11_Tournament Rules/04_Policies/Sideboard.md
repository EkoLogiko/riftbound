In certain competitions, [[Player|players]] may have a group of additional cards (outside of their starting configuration) called a **Sideboard**.

**Sideboard** size and contents are dictated by the [[Competition Format]].

Limits on copies of named cards as defined by [[Competition Format]] apply to the combination of [[Main Deck]] and **Sideboard**.

[[Player|Players]] may use **Sideboard** cards to modify their deck in between [[Game|games]] of a [[Match]]. **Sideboard** cards must be exchanged 1 for 1 with [[Main Deck]] cards.
- During that time, a [[Player|player]] may change their [[Chosen Champion]] to one from their **Sideboard** or [[Main Deck]] that meets the deckbuilding rules of the [[Competition Format]].
- In [[Constructed Format]], a [[Player|player]] may not change their [[Rune|Runes]], [[Champion Legend|Legend]], or [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] at any point after [[Deck Registration]].
- After the **sideboarding** process, a [[Player|player]]’s [[Main Deck]] must meet deck size requirements dictated by the [[Competition Format]].

[[Player|Players]] may not use **Sideboard** cards in the first [[Game]] of a [[Match]].

[[Player|Players]] **may count the cards in their opponents’** **sideboards** at any time.

A [[Player|player]] **may look at their own** **Sideboard** at any time, but must keep it distinguishable from other cards.

A [[Player|player]]’s deck and **sideboard** must be returned to their registered states before the first [[Game]] of the next [[Match]].

If a [[Match]] **completely skips the first [[Game|game]]** due to a [[Penalty]], neither [[Player|player]] may **sideboard** for the following [[Game]].

**If a [[Game]] ends in a draw**, neither [[Player|player]] may **sideboard** for the following [[Game|game]].

**If a [[Game]] restarts** due to an in-game effect or due to corrective action by [[Judge Role|Judges]], the state of each player’s deck and **Sideboard** must remain the same in the restarted [[Game|game]].
