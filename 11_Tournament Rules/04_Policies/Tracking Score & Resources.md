## Score
All [[Player|Players]] within a game are responsible for tracking all players’ scores throughout the game.

Changes to score should be announced and acknowledged by all players.
Scores must be tracked, displayed, and updated legibly in **writing**. At [[Low OPL]], an electronic device, dice, or similar methods can be used to track score.

If any player notices a score discrepancy, they must immediately make all players aware of it. If it can’t be resolved easily by the players, a [[Judge Role|Judge]] must be called.
## Resources
[[Player|Players]] must track their own resources such as [[XP]] and unspent [[Energy]]/[[Power]] they have floating. They are not required to be written down, but they must be represented in a way that is clear and understandable to all players, such as with dice or counters.

Resources being immediately spent need to be communicated, but do not need to be tracked in this way.

If any player notices a resource discrepancy, they must immediately make all players aware of it. If it can’t be resolved easily by the players, a [[Judge Role|Judge]] must be called.
