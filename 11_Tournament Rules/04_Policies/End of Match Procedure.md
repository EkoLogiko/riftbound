A [[Match]] is complete once a match result is recorded by [[Player|players]] or a tournament [[Official Role|official]].

If the time limit for a competition round is reached before a winner is determined, the player whose turn it is finishes their turn, then:
1) After that player’s turn is complete, **three additional turns** are played, and then the match ends.
2) If the [[Game]] is incomplete at the end of the additional turns, a player is declared the winner of the game if they have a **point lead of two or more**. **If no player has a point lead of two or more, the game is a draw**.

**Additional turns are untimed**, but at [[Professional OPL]] a [[Judge Role|Judge]] must be present to ensure a reasonable pace of play.

If the players are between games when the round time ends, no new game is started.

The winner of a [[Match]] is the first player who reaches the appropriate [[Game]] win score. If neither player has reached that score by the end of the end of match procedure, **the player with more game wins wins the match**.
**If players are tied on game wins, the match is a draw.**
# During Single Elimination
Single elimination matches use untimed rounds so players can play their games to a natural conclusion. In the event that single elimination rounds must use a timer, the following altered **End of Match Procedure** can be used to determine a winner if time runs out:
- **In between games**:
	- If a player has more game wins in the match --> They win the [[Match]].
	- If players have the same game wins --> A new [[Game]] starts with 5 additional turn after first turn.
- **During a match:**
	1) The player whose turn is finishes their turn.
	2) Three more additional turn are played.
	3) At the conclusion of the third additional turn:
		- **A player has the most points** --> They win that [[Game]].
		- **No player has the most points**
			- **A player has most game wins** --> They win the [[Match]].
			- **No player has the most game wins** --> First player who [[Scoring|Scores]] a point wins the [[Match]]. This does not remove normal scoring restrictions due to the **Final Point**.
