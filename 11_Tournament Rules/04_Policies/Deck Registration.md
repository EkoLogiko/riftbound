[[Player|Players]] in competitions are required to **register their decks** and [[Sideboard]] (if applicable to the format) at [[High OPL]]. The [[Head Judge]] may require **deck registration** at [[Low OPL]] too.

A **registered decklist** must have a complete, unambiguous record of the player’s [[Champion Legend]], [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], [[Main Deck]] including [[Chosen Champion]], [[Rune Deck]], and [[Sideboard]] (if applicable to the format).

Once a decklist has been registered and delivered to a competition [[Official Role|official]], **it can’t be changed.**

In a [[Constructed Format|Constructed]] event, a [[Player|player]]’s deck must be registered prior to the start of the first round of competition.

Other player’s decklists are normally considered [[Private Information]]. The [[Head Judge]] of an event may run that event or the final rounds of that event using an [[Open Decklist]] policy. In this case, all players’ decklists will be considered [[Public Information]].
# Deck Size
In a [[Constructed Format|Constructed]] event, [[Player|players]] must register a [[Main Deck]] of **exactly 40** cards (including a [[Chosen Champion]]), 1 [[Champion Legend|Legend]], 12 [[Rune|runes]], and exactly 3 [[Battlefield|battlefields]] each with a unique name.
Otherwise, [[Deck Building]] is defined in the *Core Rules*.
