The [[Setup Process]] for a [[Game]] of *Riftbound* is defined in the *Core Rules*. Additionally:
- In competitions where [[Sideboard|Sideboards]] are allowed, [[Player|players]] may exchange cards from their [[Main Deck]] and their [[Sideboard]] after each game of a [[Match]].
- If a [[Game|game]] ends in a draw, players must use the same [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] for the following game.
- Each player must [[Shuffling|shuffle]] their [[Main Deck]] and their [[Rune Deck]].
- Each player must present their [[Main Deck]], [[Rune Deck]], and [[Sideboard]] (if applicable to the format) to their opponents.
- Each player may [[Shuffling|shuffle]] the [[Main Deck]] and [[Rune Deck]] of each of their opponents.
	- In *team competitions*, each player may [[Shuffling|shuffle]] the [[Main Deck]] and [[Rune Deck]] of **one** of their team’s opponents.
	- At [[Professional OPL]], this is **required**.
	- A player may request a [[Judge Role|Judge]] [[Shuffling|shuffle]] their deck in lieu of an opponent, to be honored at [[Judge Role|Judge]] discretion.
- For the **first game** of a [[Game|game]], start of [[Game|game]] procedures may be performed as soon as both players reach their assigned seat, even before the start of the round clock. Gameplay must wait for the round clock to begin.
# Best of 1 Variation
For competitions run using a *“best of 1”* format instead of a “best of 3” format, the [[Head Judge]] may opt to use the altered initial setup process listed below to closer mirror the “best of 3” experience:
1) Each [[Player|player]] separates their [[Champion Legend]] and places it in the [[Legend Zone]].
2) Each player randomly selects one of their three [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], and places it into the [[Battlefield Zone]].
3) Players use the [[Play First Rule]] listed below to determine the designated player.
4) The designated player chooses if they would like to go first or last.
5) Players then have the opportunity to [[Sideboard]] as if they were in between games of a “best of 3” match.
6) After [[Sideboard|sideboarding]] is complete, each player separates their [[Chosen Champion]] and places it in the [[Champion Zone]].
7) Players perform [[Shuffling]] steps, then proceed to [[Setup Process]].
