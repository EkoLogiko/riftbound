[[Player|Players]] may use plastic **sleeves** or other protective devices on their cards. If a player chooses to do this, all such sleeves **must be identical** and **all cards in a deck must be placed in sleeves** in the same manner. Cards in decks **must have the same number of layers and be the same orientation** at all times.

It’s suggested but not required that players use different sleeves for the different decks ([[Main Deck]], [[Rune Deck]]).

Players may use a unique sleeve or additional layer to distinguish their [[Chosen Champion]] during game setup, but must change its sleeve to match their [[Main Deck]] if it would enter a zone whose information level is [[Private Information]] during gameplay.

Sleeves must have opaque backs.

During a [[Match]], a player may request that a [[Judge Role|Judge]] inspect an opponent’s sleeves.
[[Judge Role|Judges]] may disallow any specific sleeve or the sleeves generally if they believe they are marked, worn, inappropriate, offensive, or otherwise interfere with [[Shuffling]] or play.
# [[Organized Play Level|OPL]] Differences
- [[High OPL]], using sleeves for the [[Main Deck]], [[Rune Deck]] and [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] is a requirement. Sleeves must also do not:
	- Have highly reflective backs.
	- Contain holographic patterns on the front or back.
- [[Low OPL]], using sleeves can be required at the discretion of the [[Head Judge]].
