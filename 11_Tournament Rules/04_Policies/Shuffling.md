Decks must be made **random** at the start of each [[Game]], and whenever an instruction from cards or [[Official Role|Tournament Officials]] require it.

**Random** is defined as a state where no [[Player]] could have any information about the order or position of cards in any part of the deck.

Shuffling by methodically placing cards into distinct piles (informally known as *“pile shuffling”*) may be used as a method to count the deck, but **can’t solely be used** to make a deck **random**.

When a deck is made **random**, it is presented to the player’s opponent. This signals that the player believes their deck is legal and random.

[[Player|Players]] must notify a [[Judge Role|Judge]] if they believe their opponents’ deck has not been made sufficiently random. (see [[Insufficient Randomization]])

Players may shuffle their opponents’ decks additionally.

Players must take care not to damage cards or [[Sleeves]] through this process of shuffling, nor cause cards or sleeves to become [[Marked Cards|marked]].

When two or more cards are [[Recycle|Recycled]] to the player’s [[Main Deck]] as part of a single action, the above procedures should be followed for that group of cards to properly randomize them before [[Recycle|Recycling]] them.
