In the course of determining standings, the [[Scorekeeper]] might need to distinguish between two [[Player|players]] with equal [[Match]] records. In these cases, the following **Tiebreakers** should be applied in order:
1) The player with higher opponents’ mean (average) match win percentage.
2) The player with higher game win percentage.
3) The player with higher opponents’ mean (average) game win percentage.
4) Random method.
