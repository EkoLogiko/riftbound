At any point during a [[Match]], a [[Player]] may pause to flag a [[Floor Judge]].

Time extensions should be granted if the total time of a **Judge Call** is more than 1 minute.
# Appeals
If a [[Player]] disagrees with a [[Floor Judge]]’s ruling, they may appeal to the [[Head Judge]].

At [[High OPL]], the [[Head Judge]] may designate certain [[Floor Judge]] to be **Appeals Judges**, who are then empowered to hear and rule on appeals. In such cases, those judges must wear uniforms matching the [[Head Judge]]’s uniform.

[[Player|Players]] may not **appeal** before a full ruling is made by a [[Floor Judge]].
[[Player|Players]] may not **appeal** a ruling of an **Appeals Judge** or the [[Head Judge]].

If a penalty is applied outside the scope of a competition, the **appeals** process should follow what’s laid out on the official *Riot Games* website.
