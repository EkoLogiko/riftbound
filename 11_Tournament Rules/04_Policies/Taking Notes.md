[[Player|Players]] may take written notes during a match and refer to those notes during games in the same [[Match]].

At the beginning of each match, each player’s written note sheet **must be empty and visible to all opponents and [[Official Role|Tournament Official]].

Players **do not** have to reveal their written notes to opponents. [[Judge Role|Judges]] **may ask** players to reveal and/or explain their notes.

Players **may not** refer to notes from *outside* the current [[Match]], including notes from previous matches, during games.

Between games, players **may** refer to notes they made *prior* to the [[Match]]. They do not have to reveal these notes to opponents.
