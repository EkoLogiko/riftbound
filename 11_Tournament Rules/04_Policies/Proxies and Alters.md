## Proxies
A **proxy** is a stand-in, unofficial version of an actual *Riftbound* card. **Proxies** are **not allowed** at competitions of any [[Organized Play Level|OPL]].
The *only exception* is an official proxy issued by the [[Head Judge]] at their discretion to replace a card deemed unusable.
## Alters
An **alter** is an official *Riftbound* card that has been modified in some way. **Alters** are **allowed** in competition only if the modifications do not:
- Modify or obscure the card name or cost, or make the art unrecognizable.
- Contain substantial strategic advice.
- Contain offensive images or text.

By [[Judge Role|Judge]] discretion, **alters** may not be allowed to be used during matches that are streamed or otherwise broadcast (see [[Coverage]]).
[[Player|Players]] using **altered** cards are responsible for having official unaltered versions of those cards to use during gameplay that is streamed or otherwise broadcast if instructed by a [[Judge Role|Judge]]. When instructed, players must replace the **altered** versions with the official unaltered versions before gameplay begins for a match that is streamed or otherwise broadcast.
Alters containing material outside of *Riot Games* intellectual property may not be permitted during matches that are streamed or otherwise broadcast.

The [[Head Judge]] of a competition is the final decider on whether a card is acceptable for a competition.
