[[Player|Players]] are responsible during competitions to make sure their cards and/or sleeves are not **marked**.

**Marked** is defined as bearing something that makes it possible to identify the card without seeing its face.

If cards are sleeved, they should be inspected for marking while in the sleeves.

[[Judge Role|Judges]] may ask players to remove sleeves or replace any specific sleeve at any time during a competition.
