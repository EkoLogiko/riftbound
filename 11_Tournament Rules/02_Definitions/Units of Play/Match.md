A **Match** of *Riftbound* consists of a series of [[Game|games]] that are played until one side has won a predetermined number of [[Game|games]].

Most **matches** of *Riftbound* are “best of 3,” meaning the predetermined number of [[Game|games]] to win is 2.

Games that end in a *draw* do not count toward this goal.

If the competition round ends (due to time limits) prior to any side achieving the goal, the winner of the **match** is **the one who has won the most games**.

If all sides have equal [[Game|game]] wins, the **match** is a *draw*.
