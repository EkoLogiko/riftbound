**Competitive OPL** is a [[High OPL]]. These competitions have significant rewards, which may include invitations or advantages at the [[Professional OPL|Professional]] level.

[[Player|Players]] are expected to know the rules as defined in the *Core Rules* and this document.

All attendees are expected to maintain the integrity of the competition, while recognizing that some [[Player|players]] may not be used to playing at the highest and most technically precise level of play.

Generally, violations at this level have stricter [[Penalty|penalties]].
