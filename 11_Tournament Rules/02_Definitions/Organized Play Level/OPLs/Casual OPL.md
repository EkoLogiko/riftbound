**Causal OPL** is a [[Low OPL]]. Most competitions are **casual** and are focused on fun and social interaction, not rules enforcement.

[[Player|Players]] are expected to know most of the rules as defined in the *Core Rules*.

[[Player|Players]] are still responsible for playing according to the rules in the *Core Rules* and this document, but:
- The focus of [[Judge Role|Judges]] and competition [[Official Role|officials]] is on education and sportsmanship.
- There is no priority placed on technically precise play

Generally, violations at this level have lower [[Penalty|penalties]].
