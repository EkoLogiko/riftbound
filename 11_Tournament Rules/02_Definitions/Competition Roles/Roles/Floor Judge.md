**Floor Judge** is an [[Official Role]] and a [[Judge Role]].

**Floor Judges** respond to [[Player|players]] and [[Spectator|spectators]] who have questions about rules and regulations, and/or need judgment on the same, and their responsibilities include:
- Answering questions about rules, interactions among cards, providing *Watcher* wordings, or derived game state.
	- At [[High OPL]], **judges** may not answer direct questions about the results of [[Game Action|game actions]] a player has not yet taken. In these situations, [[Player|players]] must ask questions about the rules.
		- *Examples*:
			- <span style="color:red"><b>Inappropriate question:</b> "If I attack and conquer this battlefield with my Tryndamere, Barbarian, will I score the winning point?"</span>
			- <span style="color:green"><b>Appropriate question:</b> “Can point scoring abilities like Tryndamere’s score you the winning point?”</span>
			- **Judges** may ask [[Player|players]] to rephrase their questions to be appropriate where possible.
- Provide rulings related to game and policy violations brought to their attention.
- Assist with translation if able.
- Assist players in technically precise play (at [[Low OPL]]). *(This should generally not include strategic advice.)*
