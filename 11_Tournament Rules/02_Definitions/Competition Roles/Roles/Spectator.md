Any person present at a competition but not in one of the other roles is a **spectator**. A [[Player]] who is at a competition but not currently playing a match is also a **spectator**.

**Spectators** have responsibilities, including:
- Remaining silent during matches and anywhere else in the venue where silence is required.
- At [[Casual OPL|Casual]] and [[Competitive OPL|Competitive]] [[Organized Play Level|OPL]], **spectators** can ask [[Player|players]] to pause play while they alert a [[Judge Role|Judge]] of a potential issue.
- At [[Professional OPL]], **spectators** can’t ask [[Player|players]] to pause play, but they can still alert a [[Judge Role|Judge]] of a potential issue.

**Spectator** are *prohibited* from the following:
- Personally indicating rules or policy violations to [[Player|players]].
- Providing strategic advice to [[Player|players]].
- At [[Professional OPL]], spectators may not ask players to pause play while they call a matter to a [[Judge Role|Judge]]’s attention.

[[Player|Players]] may request to a [[Judge Role|Judge]] that a **spectator** not observe their match, at that [[Judge Role|Judge]]’s discretion.
- Players can’t request this for strategic reasons.
- At non-local competitions, there may be additional rules for **spectator** behavior and presence.
- Competition [[Official Role|officials]] may instruct that a **spectator** not observe a match.
