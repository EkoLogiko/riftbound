**Scorekeeper** is an [[Official Role]].

All sanctioned competitions require a **scorekeeper**. **Scorekeepers** are responsible for correct competition logistics as it relates to games and game records, including:
- Generating each round of the competition and necessary data to proceed with that round (e.g. match pairings).
- Generating standings in the competition *before* the last round of the competition (in [[Swiss Round Structure|Swiss]] competitions).
- Generating standings in the competition *after* the last round of the competition (in [[Swiss Round Structure|Swiss]] competitions).
- Solving scorekeeping problems in conjunction with the [[Head Judge]].
- Reporting on the competition afterward and including all necessary data.
