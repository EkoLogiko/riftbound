**Conquer** means a player gains [[Control]] of a [[Battlefield]] they did not yet [[Scoring|Score]] this turn.

A player will gain control of a [[Battlefield]] after establishing [[Control]].
# Multiplayer
In [[Mode of Play|Modes of Play]] with teammates, [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] under the [[Control]] of a teammate during the scoring step of the [[Beginning Phase]] of a player’s turn are also disqualified from being [[Scoring|Scored]] through **Conquer** by any means by that player’s team.
