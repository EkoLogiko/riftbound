There are multiple methods of playing Riftbound. A **Mode of Play** must define several variables for the game.

- **Number of Players:** How many people are playing the game.
- **Formation of Players:** How the players are organized while playing. This defines if a player is alone or on a [[Team]] and the number of opponents.
- **Victory Score:** The point total a player or team must reach to win. [[Victory Score]] can be any positive number.
- **Battlefield Count:** Determines how many [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] are in play, contributed by players, during play. This may influence deck building requirements in competitive settings This may involve utilizing less [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] than there are players.
- **Setup:** Any changes to initial setup required for this mode.
- **Format:** Conditions to win or additional rules added over play.
- **First Turn Process:** Adjustments to each player's first turn.
- **Unique Rules** (if any)
