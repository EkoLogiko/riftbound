A **Discretionary Action** is a [[Game Action]] that may be performed at any time during a player's turn during a [[Neutral State]] and [[Open State]].
A player may take **any number** of Discretionary Actions available to them during their turn, as long as:
- Any conditions, if applicable, are met.
- Any [[Cost|Costs]], if applicable, are paid.
- It does not result in any forbidden action being taken or game state being created.

>*Example*: A player can't use the [[Standard Move]] discretionary action to move a unit to a Battlefield that's already occupied by Units controlled by two other players.
