**Healing** is a [[Limited Action]] that clears [[Damage]] from one or more [[Unit|Units]]. If Damage is cleared **for any reason it is considered Healing.**

More than one [[Unit]] can be Healed at the same time.

Players may only Heal units when game effects instruct them to, or at specific points in the turn:
- During a [[Combat Cleanup]].
- At the [[Ending Phase]].
