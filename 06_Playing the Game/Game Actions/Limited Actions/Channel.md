**Channeling** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in taking one or more [[Rune|Runes]] from the top of a player's [[Rune Deck]] and putting them on [[The Board]]. A player **Channels** two Runes during the [[Channel Phase]] on their turn. Players may also Channel runes when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so.

The [[Game Effect]] that instructs a player to **channel** 1 or more runes may specify the conditions or circumstances under which those runes enter [[The Board]]. By default, runes are channeled [[Ready|readied]].

>*Example*: A spell reads **"Channel 1 rune exhausted."** As that spell resolves, its controller puts the top rune of their rune deck onto the board and that rune enters the board exhausted rather than ready.

If there aren’t sufficient runes in the [[Rune Deck]], channel as many as possible.

This action is formatted as *"Channel X rune(s),"* optionally followed by conditions or stipulations.
