**Stunning** is a [[Limited Action]] which consists in the act of selecting one or more [[Unit|Units]] on [[The Board]] and rendering them **Stunned**. Players may only **Stun** [[Unit|Units]] when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

Stunned **is a binary state**. A [[Unit]] is **Stunned** or it isn't. Moreover, a Stunned [[Unit]] **can not be Stunned again**.

>*Example*: Eclipse Herald has the ability **"When you stun an enemy unit, ready me and give me +1 [M] this turn."** Its controller plays a spell that reads "Stun a unit." They may choose a unit that's already stunned, but if they do, Eclipse Herald will not trigger.

Stunned Units lose the Stunned status during *step 3* of the [[End of Turn Cleanup]].

A Stunned Unit does not contribute its [[Might]] to [[Damage]] in the [[Combat Damage Step]].

A Stunned Unit must still have [[Damage]] applied to it equal to, or greater than, its full [[Might]] value to be [[Kill|killed]].
