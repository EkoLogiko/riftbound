**Attaching** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in linking two cards on [[The Board]] together to combine their effects in some way. This causes one or more cards to become [[Attached]] and at least one card to become a [[Top-Most Card]]. Players may only Attach cards when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

**Attaching** affects two cards at once:
- One card will be designated as the one being **Attached**, and thus become [[Attached]].
- The other will become the [[Top-Most Card]].

This is represented by physically laying the [[Top-Most Card]] on top of the other or others such that all [[Effect Text|Effect Texts]] and [[Might Bonus]] are showing, but nothing else from the card or cards [[Attached]]. In the situation where there is more than one card attached to the [[Top-Most Card]], they should be stacked in such a way that all [[Effect Text]] boxes and [[Might Bonus|Might Bonuses]] are readable. The order of the [[Attached]] cards has no bearing on the application of effects.

Attaching a card to a new [[Top-Most Card]] will cause it to [[Detach]] from the card to which it is currently Attached.

Attaching a card to its current [[Top-Most Card]] will not have any effect.

If a [[Game Effect]] instructs a player to Attach a card to its current [[Top-Most Card]], nothing additional happens.

Attaching cards does not inherently choose or specify a [[Target]]. However, Game Effects that Attach cards may do so.

When a card Attaches to a card, its location becomes the same as the new [[Top-Most Card]]. This is not a [[Move]].

Attaching a card to another card does not modify either card’s state except in making those cards [[Attached]] and the [[Top-Most Card]], and causing the Attached card to change locations.

>*Example*: An exhausted equipment being attached to a unit does not ready the equipment.
