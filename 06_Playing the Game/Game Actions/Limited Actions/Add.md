Adding is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in putting resources into a player's [[Rune Pool]]. Players may only Add resources when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so.

[[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] and [[Activated Abilities]] that **Add** resources [[Finalize]] immediatly when they are played, even when handling [[Task|Tasks]] or during the [[Resolution]] of [[Spell|spells]] and [[Ability|abilities]], and resolve as soon as they are [[Finalize|Finalized]].
[[Priority]] and [[Focus]] will not pass from **Add** abilities being finalized or resolving. Any ongoing [[Task]] or step in the [[FEPR]] process is paused while they are being [[Finalize|Finalized]] and while they are resolving.

[[Spell|Spells]] that **Add** resources **will be [[Finalize|Finalized]] and will resolve at the normal timing for spells.**

[[Activated Abilities]] that **Add** resources and have the [[Reaction]] [[Tag]] can be activated at **any time** that spells or abilities require resources be paid. When an Add ability is activated in this way, **it immediately finalizes and resolves**, even during the resolution of spells and abilities.

>*Example*: A player can add [[Energy]] and [[Power]] through any means before initiating the process of playing a spell. After initiating that process, in the Pay Costs step, they may activate Add Reactions to add Energy or Power to pay costs.
>*Example*: A player moves two units to a battlefield where their opponent controls a Mageseeker Investigator. Although they have no window of priority, they may activate Add Reactions to pay for the applied cost from Mageseeker Investigator, and those abilities finalize and resolve immediately.

This action is formatted as *"Add [one or more resources]."*
