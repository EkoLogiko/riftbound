**Buffing** is a [[Limited Action]] which consists in placing a **Buff** [[Counters|Counter]] on a [[Unit]]. A **Buff** is also a [[Game Object|game object]]. To **Buff** a unit, place a **Buff** Counter on it if it does not have one already (If the unit already has a Buff Counter on it, it does not get another one). Players may only **Buff** units when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so.

Some effects may grant a [[Game Object]] permission to be **Buffed** multiple times.

[[Unit|Units]] with Buff Counters **can still be chosen** for actions that Buff units, but will not be Buffed as part of the execution.

This action is formatted as *"Buff [one or more units]."*
