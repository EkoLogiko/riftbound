**Doubling** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in increasing a numeric attribute by an amount equal to that attribute’s current value. Players may only **Double** when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so.

This creates an effect that modulates that attribute by that specific amount for the duration specified by the [[Game Effect]] that instructed the player to perform this action.

>*Example*: A unit with 3 base Might and Shield 2 is in combat as a Defender. Since Shield applies, its current Might is 5. A player chooses it as the target for Last Stand, a spell that reads in part **“Double a friendly unit's Might this turn.”** Its current Might is 5, so it gets +5 Might this turn, for a current Might of 10. After combat, Shield no longer applies, but the +5 Might from Last Stand does, so the unit’s Might is 8.

Some [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] may also cause [[Damage]] to be **Doubled**; in this case, those effects are the equivalent to a [[Replacement Effect]] that reads *“If I would take damage from a source, I take double that damage from that source instead.”* or *“If [I/this] would deal damage, [I/this] deal double that damage instead.”* The source of the Damage in these cases remains the same after Doubling.
