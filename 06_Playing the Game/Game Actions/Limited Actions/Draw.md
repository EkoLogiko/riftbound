**Drawing** is a [[Limited Action]] which takes a single card from a zone and adds it to the player's [[Hand]]. Unless specified otherwise, drawing takes cards from the top of the [[Main Deck]].

Each player draws 1 during the [[Draw Phase]] on their turn. The player may draw cards when instructed to do so by other game effects.

This action, when instructed, is formatted as *"Draw X."*

If a player attempts to draw more cards than are available in their [[Main Deck]], they do the following:
1) Draw as many as possible.
2) Perform a [[Burning Out|Burn Out]].
3) Draw the remaining cards needed to complete the **Draw** action.
