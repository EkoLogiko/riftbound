**Replacing** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in [[Create|Creating]] a [[Token]] in the place of another card or token without playing it while inheriting all effects or statuses of the [[Game Object]] it replaced. The player may **Replace** cards and tokens when instructed to do so by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

The **replacing** token is treated as the same [[Game Object]] as the card or token it replaced for the purposes of [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] that [[Target]] or reference that game object.

>*Example*: A player with Green Father as their legend conquers Navori Fighting Pit. They choose to place the Green Father conquer effect on the chain after the Navori Fighting Pit conquer effect. When the Green Father trigger resolves, Navori Fighting Pit is replaced with Brush. Although the Navori Fighting Pit has been replaced, the “here” in its triggered ability still can have its information referenced, because the Brush inherited all statuses and conditions. The unit Navori Fighting Pit’s triggered ability has targeted will still be a legal target on resolution

This action, when instructed, is formatted as *"Replace [X] with [Y]."* The [X] is the target to be **Replaced**. The [Y] is the object that will **Replace** the target. This will always specify a [[Token]] to create.

Replacing **is not** a subset of [[Banish|Banishing]]. The card or token that is Replaced is placed in [[Banishment]]. While it resides in [[Banishment]], it is considered to have been [[Replace|Replaced]] and not [[Banish|Banished]].

If a **token is Replaced** it will stop existing once it begins its occupancy in [[Banishment]]. This **does not invalidate** the token created, or the act of **Replacement**.
# Swapping Back
Tokens that have been [[Create|Created]] through a **Replace** action can be instructed to be *“Swapped back.”* This may also appear as **“replace [the token] with the [Game Object] it replaced.”**

**Swapping Back** is an extension of the Replace action.

To **Swap Back**, the [[Token]] stops existing and the original card is returned to the space that the token just occupied, inheriting all current effects and statuses.

Any card that has been Replaced by that token or any tokens it Replaced is eligible to swap back in this way.

If there is nothing in [[Banishment]] to swap back to then this object can never swap back.
