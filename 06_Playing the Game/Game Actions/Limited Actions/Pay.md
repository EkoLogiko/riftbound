**Paying** a resource is a[[Limited Action]] consisting in removing that resource from your [[Rune Pool]]. Pay is an action that interacts with [[Energy]] and [[Power]]. Players may only [[Pay]] resources when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

When a player is instructed to **Pay** a resource, that player may remove that resource from their [[Rune Pool]] if it exists there. If they choose not to, the instruction is ignored.

>*Example*: When Diana, Lunari’s triggered ability resolves, if its controller has [1] Energy in their rune pool, they choose whether or not to pay that Energy as instructed. If they choose not to pay the Energy, they will not perform the additional effects of the triggered ability.

If the instruction to **Pay** resources is part of paying [[Cost|costs]] for a card or ability, choosing not to pay those resources will cause the act of playing the card or ability to be undone. (see [[Check Legality]])

If the instruction to **Pay** resources is part of paying any other [[Cost|cost]], choosing not to pay those resources will prevent the execution of the associated effect.

Players may activate abilities that [[Add]] resources with the [[Reaction]] keyword at any time that they are instructed to **Pay** resources. Those abilities [[Finalize]] and resolve immediately, ignoring normal restrictions.
