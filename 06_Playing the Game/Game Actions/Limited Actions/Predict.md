**Predicting** a card is a [[Limited Action]] consting in looking at a single card from the top of the [[Main Deck]] and choosing whether or not to [[Recycle]] it. When more than one card is **Predicted**, the **Predicting** player looks at that many cards and [[Recycle|Recycles]] any number of them before putting the rest back on top of their [[Main Deck]] in any order. Players may only Predict cards when instructed to do so by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

This action, when instructed, is formatted as *"Predict X."* If omitted, X is presumed to be 1.

If a player attempts to Predict more cards than are available in their [[Main Deck]], they will Predict as many as possible instead. The Player will not perform a [[Burning Out|Burn Out]] as a result of Predicting with too few cards in their deck.
