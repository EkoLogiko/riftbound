**Disempowering** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in removing the [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]] status from one or more [[Game Object|Game Objects]]. Players may only **Disempower**[[Game Object|Game Objects]] when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

**Disempowering** affects only cards that are currently [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]]. Game effects that instruct a player to **Disempower** a card that is not currently [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]] **will do nothing.**
