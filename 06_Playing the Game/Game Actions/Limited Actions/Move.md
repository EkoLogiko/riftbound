**Moving** is a [[Limited Action]] and is the act of a [[Game Object]] moving between two [[Location|Locations]] on [[The Board]].

Players may only move [[Game Object|Game Objects]] when instructed to do so by Game Effects or costs.

The [[Standard Move]] inherent to [[Unit|Units]] may also cause **Movement**.
