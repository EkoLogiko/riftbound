**Burning** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in moving cards from the top of a player’s [[Main Deck]] to their [[Trash]]. A player must **Burn** cards when instructed to do so by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] or [[Cost|costs]].

*“When you burn”* abilities or other [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] that trigger on burning are executed after burning has occurred.

When **Burning** is part of an effect, then a player must **Burn** as many cards as possible. If instructed to burn more cards than they have in their [[Main Deck]], they burn that many cards, [[Burning Out|Burn Out]] and then burn the rest.

This action is formatted as *"Burn X."* X is the number of cards to be **Burned**.
