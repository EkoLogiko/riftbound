**Countering** is a [[Limited Action]] and the act of negating the execution, activation, or otherwise [[Playing a Card]] or [[Playing or Activating Abilities]] by a player. A card or ability that is **Countered** does nothing and is cleared from [[The Chain]]. Cards that are cleared from [[The Chain]] in this way are placed in the [[Trash]]. Players may only Counter cards and abilities when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

A card that is Countered **is not considered to have been [[Play|played]]** for [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] on cards being played.

Countering does not refund any [[Cost|Costs]] paid to play a card, activate an ability, or trigger an ability. This includes [[Additional Cost|Additional Costs]].

This action is formatted as "Counter [a card or ability on the chain]."
