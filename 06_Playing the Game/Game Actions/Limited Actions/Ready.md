**Readying** is a [[Limited Action]] that marks a non-[[Spell|spell]] [[Game Object]] on [[The Board]] as available for action. To mark it, rotate the card 90 degrees opposite of [[Exhaust|Exhausting]], so that it is vertically oriented in front of you.

A [[Game Object]] that is already Ready **cannot be Readied again**. If a [[Game Object]] is instructed to be Readied while it is already Ready, **nothing additional happens**.

"Ready" is a state for [[Game Object|Game Objects]] on the board that other game effects and rules can reference.

A player Readies all non-[[Spell|spell]] [[Game Object|Game Objects]] they [[Control]] during the [[Awaken Phase]] on their turn. Players may also Ready [[Game Object|Game Objects]] on the board when effects or spells instruct them to do so.
