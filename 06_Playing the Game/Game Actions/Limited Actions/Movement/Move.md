**Moving** is a [[Limited Action]] and is the act of a [[Game Object]] moving between two [[Location|Locations]] on [[The Board]]. Players may only move [[Game Object|Game Objects]] when instructed to do so by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] or [[Cost|costs]].

The [[Standard Move]] inherent to [[Unit|Units]] may also cause **Movement**. Players may choose to move their Units with the [[Standard Move]].

A [[Permanent]] changing its position from any space on **[[The Board]] to another space on [[The Board]]** is a Move, unless it is caused by a corrective [[Recall]] or an [[Attached]] Permanent changing locations to or with its [[Top-Most Card]].

A card changing game zones does not in itself constitute a **Move**.

**Moving** is **instantaneous**:
- There is no state for [[Permanent|Permanents]] between locations.
- Permanents are either at their [[Origin]] before Moving or their [[Destination]] after Moving.
- Moving does not use [[The Chain]], nor is it able to be [[Reaction|reacted]] to.

**Moving** is defined by the [[Origin]] and [[Destination]] of the [[Permanent]] that is changing locations.

[[Spell|Spells]], [[Ability|Abilities]], or other effects may cause a Move to occur. The source of the Move will provide details on any restrictions on legality for [[Destination]]. Units cannot Move to a [[Battlefield]] that already has units from 2 other players present by any means.

The [[Destination]] becomes [[Contested Control|Contested]] if it is an **Uncontested Battlefield** not [[Control|Controlled]] by the controller of the [[Unit]] or [[Unit|Units]] that moved.

[[Unit|Units]] may cause either a [[Non-Combat Showdown]] or a [[Combat]] when they Move.

When a Move action is complete, perform a [[Cleanup]].
