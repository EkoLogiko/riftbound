The **Destination** is where the [[Permanent]] is going to during [[Move|Movement]].
# Multiplayer
In [[Mode of Play|Modes of Play]] with more than two players, [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] with Staged [[Combat|Combats]] or [[Combat|Combats]] in Progress are **Invalid Destinations** for [[Move|Moves]] of all kinds ([[Standard Move]] or otherwise) by [[Unit|Units]] controlled by Players not involved in those [[Combat|Combats]] or who don't already have units at that [[Battlefield]].

In [[Mode of Play|Modes of Play]] with teammates, [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] occupied by a player’s teammates are Invalid Destinations for [[Move|Moves]] of all kinds ([[Standard Move]] or otherwise) by [[Unit|Units]] that player controls.

If an action would require a [[Move]] that would cause a [[Unit]] to become present in a Location where it cannot move for any reason, such as a [[Battlefield]] with two players that are not the controller of the [[Unit]] performing this [[Move]] action are in a [[Combat]] or such a [[Combat]] is Staged, it instead [[Recall|Recalls]].
# Invalid Destination
**(Unofficial)** An **Invalid Destination** that can't be chosen as a valid target destination for [[Move|Moves]] or spells and abilities that [[Move]] a [[Permanent]].
