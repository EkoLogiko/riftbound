**Detaching** is a [[Limited Action]] consisting in unlinking two cards that are currently linked through the act of [[Attach|Attaching]]. This causes one to cease being [[Attached]], and potentially causes the other to cease being a [[Top-Most Card]]. Players may only Detach cards when directed to by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

Detaching affects only cards that are currently [[Attached]] to another card. Game effects that instruct a player to **Detach** a card that is currently not Attached to anything will do nothing.

When one or more cards become **Detached**, they cease to be in the [[Attached]] state. To represent this, these cards should no longer be placed under the card they were previously placed under as [[Top-Most Card]].

The card being Detached has its [[Effect Text]] become [[Inactive]] and its [[Rules Text]] cease being [[Inactive]].

Detaching cards does not inherently choose, or specify a [[Target]]. However, Game Effects that Detach cards may.

When a card Detaches from a [[Top-Most Card]], its location is the same as the Top-Most Card from which it **Detached**.

If the Detached card was a [[Gear]] and this causes it to become present at a [[Battlefield]], it will be [[Recall|Recalled]] during the next [[Cleanup]].

If the Attached card was Detached because the Top-Most Card changed zones from [[The Board]] to a [[Non-Board Zone]], then the location that the Attached Card will Detach to is the last location the Top-Most Card was at before changing from a board zone to a non-board zone.
