A **Limited Action** is a game action that a [[Spell|spell]], [[Ability|ability]], or circumstance of the turn's progression causes the player to perform. A player cannot perform these actions at-will.
These actions can only be taken when a player is instructed to do so as the execution of an effect or during their specified occasion during the turn. 

>*Example*: A player may [[Draw]] during the Draw Phase on their turn or when instructed to do so by a spell or ability. They can't choose to [[Draw]] at any other time.
