**Hiding** is a [[Discretionary Action]] and is the act of placing a card facedown at a [[Battlefield]] you control.

The [[Hidden]] keyword allows a player to pay a cost to **Hide** a card any time they have a [[Hidden]] card in their [[Hand]] or [[Champion Zone]] and meet the prerequisites.

Cards that are facedown at [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] have their gameplay properties and permissions defined by the effect that put them there.

>*Example*: Cards that players Hide with the Hidden keyword can be Played for [0] on any subsequent turn as a property of the Hidden keyword.

If a facedown card would change zones or if the game ends, its owner reveals it to all players.
