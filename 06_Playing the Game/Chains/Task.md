A Task is one or more steps or processes that one or more Players must perform before continuing with any other actions.

Tasks include, but are not limited to: 
- [[Cleanup]]
- the actions performed during the [[Start of Turn]] process
- throughout [[Combat]] in its various steps 
- the actions performed during the [[Ending Phase]] process

Whenever a Player takes one or more actions that incur Tasks they should refer to the process of [[HOT FEPR]].
