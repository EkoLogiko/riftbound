The FEPR (Finalize Execute Pass Resolve) process determines how the players can interact with [[The Chain]]. 

In the sequence of resolving FEPR more Chain Items may become [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]]. These will be processed by the same FEPR process that produced them.

The process represents the sequence of the following steps:
1) [[Finalize]]
2) [[Execute]]
3) [[Pass]]
4) [[Resolution|Resolve]]
