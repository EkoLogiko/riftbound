Pass is the third step of [[FEPR]]. It follows the [[Execute]] step and is followed by the [[Resolution|Resolve]] step.

- If all players have passed Priority in sequence without adding any items to [[The Chain]] --> Proceed to Step 4: [[Resolution|Resolve]]. 
- Otherwise, the player with [[Priority]] passes [[Priority]] to the next Player in [[Turn Order]] --> Return to Step 2: [[Execute]].
