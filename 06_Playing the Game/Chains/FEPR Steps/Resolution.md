The Resolution of a card or and ability is the result of the Resolve step of the [[FEPR]] process.

Resolve is the last step of [[FEPR]]. It follows the [[Pass]] step.

During the Resolve step, the newest [[Finalized Chain Item]] resolves: this means the player must execute its [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] in their entirety. 

After this:
- If [[The Chain]] is empty --> Play proceeds in an [[Open State]]. 
	- If this occurs during a [[Showdown]] and the chain wasn’t initiated by a [[Triggered Ability]] or an ability that [[Add|Adds]] resources, [[Focus]] passes to the next player in [[Turn Order]]. 
- If [[The Chain]] is not empty:
	- There are one or more [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] --> return to Step 1: [[Finalize]]. 
	- There are no Pending Items --> The controller of the newest item on the chain gains [[Priority]]. Return to Step 2: [[Execute]].
