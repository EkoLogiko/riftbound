A **Chain Item** is any card or [[Ability]] which exists on [[The Chain]]. When something is put on [[The Chain]], it immediatly becomes a Chain Item in the form of a [[Pending Chain Item]].
At the end of the [[Finalize]] step of [[FEPR]], the item may become a [[Finalized Chain Item]].
After [[Resolution]], the [[Finalized Chain Item]] leaves [[The Chain]].
