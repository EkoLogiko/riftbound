A **Combat** occurs as a result of [[Unit|Units]] controlled by opposing players being present at the same [[Battlefield]]. This could be the result of a [[Standard Move]] Intrinsic Ability, a [[Spell]], or other [[Game Effect]]. The source effect does not change the structure or flow of Combat once initiated.

A Combat can only occur between two players

Play proceeds following the [[Steps of Combat]].

Combat will also include a [[Showdown]].
