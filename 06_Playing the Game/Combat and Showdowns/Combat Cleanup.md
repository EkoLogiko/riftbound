The **Combat Cleanup** is a Special [[Cleanup]] performed ad the start of the [[Resolution Step]] of the [[Steps of Combat]].

It follows all the [[Cleanup]] steps, but inserts:
	3c) [[Heal]] all [[Unit|Units]]
	3d) Recall **Attackers** present at the [[Battlefield]] if **Defenders** are still present.

A **tie** occurs when there are units controlled by different players located at a [[Battlefield]] where [[Combat]] is taking place during the step 3d of the **combat cleanup**.
