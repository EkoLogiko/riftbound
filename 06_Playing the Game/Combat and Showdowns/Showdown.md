A **Showdown** is a **Window of Opportunity** in which Players have an [[Open State]] in which they may play [[Spell|Spells]] in an alternating fashion. Each spell played this way creates a [[The Chain|Chain]] as normal.

The [[Turn States|State of Turn]] is partially determined by whether or not a **Showdown** or [[Combat]] is in progress. 
- [[Showdown State]] --> a **Showdown** or [[Combat]] is in progress. Cards of all Categories and [[Ability|abilities]], by default, cannot be played during a Showdown State.
- [[Neutral State]] if no **Showdown** or [[Combat]] is in progress.

A Showdown begins when [[Control]] of a [[Battlefield]] is [[Contested Control|Contested]] during a [[Cleanup]] and the turn is in [[Neutral State]] and [[Open State]].

# Showdown Types
Showdowns are of two types:
- [[Combat Showdown]] --> Thrown when [[Control]] of a [[Battlefield]] is [[Contested Control|Contested]] between two players. If a Showdown is already ongoing at that [[Battlefield]], it will become a [[Combat Showdown]] and a [[Combat]] will initiate there.
- [[Non-Combat Showdown]] --> Thrown if there aren’t units controlled by different players there

# Showdowns and Focus
As a **Showdown** begins, the player who applied [[Contested Control|Contested]] status to the [[Battlefield]] gains [[Focus]] (and, therefore, [[Priority]]).
When the last item on the [[The Chain|Chain]] resolves and the turn returns to an [[Open State]] during a Showdown, [[Focus]] passes, and the next Player gains [[Focus]] (and, therefore, [[Priority]]).
[[Focus]] will not pass in this way if the chain opened as a result of a [[Triggered Ability]] being added to the [[The Chain|Chain]], nor if it opened as a result of an [[Add]] ability being added to the chain.

# Player Choices during a Showdown
During a **Showdown**, the player with [[Focus]] may do one of the following:
- [[Playing a Card|Play a Card]] or [[Activated Abilities|Activated Ability]] that is legally timed --> The card or activated ability will start a [[The Chain|Chain]] as normal and, when that [[The Chain|Chain]] closes, [[Focus]] passes to the next Player in [[Turn Order]].
- Pass.
	- If all Players have passed once in sequence, the Showdown ends.
	- Otherwise, Focus passes to the next Player in Turn Order

# End of Showdowns
If all players pass [[Focus]] without playing a spell or activating an ability, then the Showdown closes. 
- If it is a [[Combat Showdown]], proceed with the remaining steps of [[Combat]] to resolve the phase.
- If it is a [[Non-Combat Showdown]], do the following: 
	- If only one player’s [[Unit|Units]] remain at the [[Battlefield]], and if that player does not already [[Control]] the [[Battlefield]], that player establishes [[Control]] over the [[Battlefield]]. This results in a [[Conquer]] if that player has not yet scored that [[Battlefield]] this turn.
