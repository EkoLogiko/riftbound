The **Combat Showdown Step** is the third and last step of the [[Steps of Combat]]. It follows the [[Combat Damage Step]].
1) As the **Resolution Step** is entered, a [[Combat Cleanup]] is performed.
2) The following [[Task]] becomes [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]]: (Reminder: Resolve any items on [[The Chain]] from [[Deal|dealing]] [[Combat Damage]] and the [[Combat Cleanup]] and associated [[FEPR]] before performing this step.)
	1) Determine [[Combat Result]].
3) The following [[Task]] becomes [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]] (Reminder: Resolve any items on [[The Chain]] from determining [[Combat Result]] and associated [[FEPR]] before performing this step.):
	1)  If no [[Showdown]] or [[Combat]] is staged at this location, the player with [[Unit|Units]] remaining here Establishes [[Control]] if they didn’t already control this [[Battlefield]]. This does not have to be the player that applied [[Contested Control|Contested]] to the [[Battlefield]].
		1) Clear the [[Contested Control|Contested]] Status.
		2) If there are no [[Unit|Units]] remaining here controlled by any player, the [[Battlefield]] becomes Uncontrolled.
		3) Remove all [[Hidden]] cards from this [[Battlefield]] that do not share a controller with the [[Battlefield]].
		4) Establishing [[Control]] results in a [[Conquer]] if that player has not yet scored this Battlefield this turn.
4) The following [[Task]] becomes [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]] (Reminder: Resolve any items on [[The Chain]] from establishing control and associated [[FEPR]] before performing this step.):
	1) [[Combat]] ends.
		1) Remove [[Attacker]] and [[Defender]] Designation from all Units and Players.
		2) "At the end of Combat" or "Combat ends" effects take place.
		3) All *“this combat”* effects expire simultaneously.
