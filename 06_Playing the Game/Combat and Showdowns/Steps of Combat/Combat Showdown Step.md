The **Combat Showdown Step** is the first step of the [[Steps of Combat]]. It is followed by the [[Combat Damage Step]].

[[Combat]] will open in one of two ways:
- When a [[Combat]] and [[Showdown]] are staged at the same [[Battlefield]] and the turn player initiates the [[Showdown]], is opens as a [[Combat Showdown]].
- When the turn is in a [[Showdown State]] and [[Open State]] and a [[Combat]] is **Staged** at the [[Battlefield]] where the current [[Showdown]] is ongoing.

The following [[Task|Tasks]] become [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]], in the order described:
1) "Start of combat" or "showdown" effects happen now.
2) Establish who is [[Attacker]] and who is [[Defender]].
	- The attacker and defender gain the **Attacker** or **Defender** designation now, as appropriate.
	- [[Unit|Units]] at the [[Contested Control|Contested]] [[Battlefield]] controlled by the [[Attacker]] or [[Defender]] gain the **Attacker** or **Defender** designation now, as appropriate.
	- If a [[Unit]] controlled by the [[Attacker]] or [[Defender]] becomes present at this [[Battlefield]] after this moment, it will gain the **Attacker** or **Defender** designation during the [[Cleanup]] phase following the action that caused it to become present, as appropriate for its controller.
3) The [[Attacker]] gains [[Focus]].
4) Add items to the [[The Chain]] (this is referred to as **Combat Chain**) if establishing [[Attacker]] and [[Defender]] has caused [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] to become [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]].
	- The Attacking player, who has [[Focus]], places [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] on the Chain first, followed by all non-Defender players in [[Turn Order]], followed by the Defending Player.

The State becomes a [[Closed State]] if a **Combat Chain** was created. Otherwise the [[Combat Showdown]] continues, with the State being an [[Open State]] as normal.

Players proceed with any play on [[The Chain]] as normal.
