The **Combat Damage Step** is the second step of the [[Steps of Combat]]. It follows the [[Combat Showdown Step]] and is followed by the [[Resolution Step]].

If both **Attacking** and **Defending** [[Unit|Units]] remain at this [[Battlefield]], the following [[Task|Tasks]] become [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]], in the specified order:
1) When the [[Showdown]] closes, [[Attacker]] and [[Defender]] resolve [[Combat Damage]] at the [[Battlefield]] that was attacked, using the current [[Might]] of their [[Unit|Units]].
2) Skip the [[FEPR]] process and cancel any [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding Tasks]]. Proceed to the [[Resolution Step]].
