During a [[Combat]], the **Attacker** is the player whose [[Unit|unit]](s) applied the [[Contested Control|Contested]] status to the [[Battlefield]].

If a [[Showdown]] opens as part of [[Combat]], this player gains [[Focus]] as the showdown begins. If a [[Showdown]] was already ongoing when the [[Combat]] opens, the player who has [[Focus]] maintains it.

The **Attacker** [[Unit|units]] gain the **Attacker Designation**.
