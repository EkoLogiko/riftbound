With **Combat Damage** it is intended all the [[Damage]] that is calulcated, assigned and then [[Deal|Dealt]] by [[Unit|Units]] during the [[Combat Damage Step]] of the [[Steps of Combat]].
# Calculations
1) Sum the [[Might]] of **Attacking** [[Unit|Units]].
2) Sum the [[Might]] of all **Defending** [[Unit|Units]].
3) Starting with the [[Attacker]], each player assigns an amount of [[Damage]] equal to their summed [[Might]] among the other's [[Unit|Units]]. (see [[Damage Assignment]])
4) [[Deal]] [[Damage]] to each unit equal to the amount assigned to it.
