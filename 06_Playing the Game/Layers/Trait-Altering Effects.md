This [[Layer]] deals with effects that grant, remove, or replace inherent traits of [[Game Object|Game Objects]].
- [[Card Name]]
- [[Supertype]]
- [[Card Type]]
- [[Tag|Tags]]
- Controller
- [[Cost]]
- [[Domain]]
- Base [[Might]]
- Base [[Might Bonus]]

This is the second [[Layer]] to be evaluated when evaluating [[Layer|Layers]].

Effects for this **layer** can be identified by the phrase *"become(s)"*, *"give,"* *"is,"* or *"are"* in the text.
>*Example*: A permanent has the ability **"Other friendly units are Yordles."** Other friendly units gain the Yordle tag in this layer.
