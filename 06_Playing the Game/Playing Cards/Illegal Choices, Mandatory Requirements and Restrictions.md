A player may not make choices during this step that will deterministically result in illegal choices or actions later in this process unless they have no choice. 
>*Example*: A player plays a card which reads “As an additional cost to play this, kill the unit you control with the most Might. Give a friendly unit +[M] equal to the killed unit’s Might this turn. Predict 2.” They cannot choose to target their unit with the highest Might during this step of finalization.
# Mandatory Requirements
If a [[Game Effect]] has introduced a **Mandatory Requirement** on any of these choices, that mandatory requirement must be followed if possible. A mandatory requirement is one that specifies what choice can be made or how that choice is made, but doesn't specifically disallow other game states.
>*Example*: Here to Help is a spell that reads in part “You may play a unit from your hand to a battlefield you control.” If its controller decides to play a unit, they must play it to a battlefield they control.
>*Example*: Rek’Sai, Swarm Queen is a unit that reads “When I attack, you may reveal the top 2 cards of your Main Deck. You may banish one, then play it. If it is a unit, you may play it here. Recycle the rest.” The “you may play it here,” portion of this ability is not a mandatory requirement, because the player isn’t required to play the unit to that location.

If there are multiple **Mandatory Requirements** that *do not conflict*, players must make a choice that will satisfy those mandatory requirements. Any individual group of such complementary mandatory requirements that contains as many non-contradictory mandatory requirements as possible forms a set.
If there are two or more sets of mandatory requirements that are *mutually contractictory*, choose one of those sets and ignore the rest.
>*Example*: If Here to Help were played from hand, and its controller controlled both battlefields, if they paid the [A][A] additional cost, they would have no choice but to play Dune Drake to Dragon Roost as it would satisfy both mandatory requirements.
>*Example*: Here to Help is played from facedown at Targon’s Peak. Its controller chooses to play a Dune Drake from hand. The other battlefield is Dragon Roost. When finalizing Dune Drake, they choose to pay the [A][A] additional cost from Dragon Roost. There are two competing mandatory requirements here: playing Dune Drake to Targon’s Peak, or playing it to Dragon Roost. Dune Drake’s controller must choose one of those requirements to satisfy, and ignore the rest.
# Restrictions
If there are one or more restrictions on choices being made during this process, those restrictions must be followed. Failure to do so means the card will fail to [[Finalize]].
>*Example*: Perched Grimwyrm is a unit that reads “Play me only to a battlefield you conquered this turn.” If a player plays Perched Grimwyrm via Here to Help and they haven’t conquered a battlefield this turn, Perched Grimwyrm will fail to finalize. Although Here to Help presents a mandatory requirement, Perched Grimwyrm’s restriction on being played overrides it.
 *Example*: Mageseeker Warden is a unit that reads in part “While I'm at a battlefield, opponents can only play units to their base.” If a player attempts to play a Perched Grimwyrm to a battlefield they’ve conquered this turn while their opponent controls a Mageseeker Warden at a battlefield, the Perched Grimwyrm will fail to finalize.
