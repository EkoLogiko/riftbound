**Playing a card** is the act of a player utilizing their cards. A card is **Played** when it has finished this process in its entirety.
Cards have different behaviors when played:
- [[Permanent|Permanents]] become [[Game Object]] when **Played**.
- [[Spell|Spells]] create [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] that are executed, then the card is placed in the [[Trash]] when **Played**. 
- [[Token|Tokens]] are not cards, but can still be **Played**.  

Cards have different states during the process of being played: When initially being played cards are Pending, as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]]. Near the end of the process cards will cease being Pending and become [[Finalized Chain Item|Finalized Chain Items]].

# The Process of Playing
1) **The card is moved on the [[The Chain|Chain]].**
	- The State becomes a [[Closed State]].
	- The card becomes a [[Pending Chain Item]].
	- If another [[Game Effect]] or [[Ability|ability]] is currently resolving, continue resolving it before proceeding with any further steps of this process.
	- If there are [[Task|Tasks]] which are [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]] or currently being handled, finish those [[Task|Tasks]] before continuing this process.
2) **Make relevant choices.**
	- If the card has an effect that specifies a choice *"As I am played,"* those choices are made now. This includes the choice of whether or not to pay an Optional [[Additional Cost]].
	- Depending on the [[Card Type]], do the following:
		- [[Unit]] --> Choose a valid [[Location]] where that Unit will enter upon being **Played**. By default, valid locations include the controller’s [[Base]] or a [[Battlefield]] the controller [[Control|controls]]. Some [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] may grant players permission to play Units to locations that are not normally valid. Such locations become valid for the purposes of **Playing** the Unit.
		- [[Spell|Spells]] and [[Ability|Abilities]]
			- If they have a bulleted list of modes to choose from, make the appropriate choices now.
			- If they [[Move]] one or more [[Unit|Units]], choose a valid [[Location]] as the [[Destination]] for each [[Move]] that will be performed. A valid [[Location]] for a [[Move]] is one other than the Units’ current [[Location]] where they are allowed to be present.
	- If a card requires you to specifically choose one or more [[Game Object|Game Objects]], that choice is made now.
		- This does not include cards that affect one or more [[Game Object|Game Objects]] based on criteria. 
		- This does not include making choices for [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] of [[Permanent|permanents]], or [[Delayed Trigger|Delayed Triggers]] or [[Reflexive Trigger|Reflexive Triggers]] generated by the item being finalized, even if those abilities trigger when the chain item is played.
	- Follow the indications of [[Target]] and [[Splitting]] to make valid choices.
	- If a [[Spell|spell]] or [[Ability|ability]] requires one or more players to make choices that are not outlined in this section, they are made on resolution.
3) **Determine [[Total Cost]].** --> Costs may be [[Energy]] costs, [[Power]] costs, or non-standard costs (like "killing an unit").
	1) Apply [[Base Cost]] modifications in any order. (See [[Base Cost]] to know more about modifications)
	2) Apply [[Additional Cost|Additional Costs]] in any order.
	3) Apply [[Cost Increases]].
	4) Apply [[Cost Discounts]].
	5) Apply [[Total Cost]] modifications in any order.
4) **Pay the [[Cost]].**
	1) In total, pay the combined [[Energy]] cost (if any) and [[Power]] cost (if any).
		- During this step, the card's controller can use [[Activated Abilities|activated abilities]] with the [[Reaction]] [[Tag]] that [[Add]] resources to add [[Energy]] and [[Power]] to pay the card's costs.
	2) In addition, pay any non-standard [[Cost]] summed in *step 3* in any order. Costs that are replaced with other events by replacement effects are still considered paid.
	- A player may not pay costs during this step that will deterministically result in illegal choices or actions later in this process unless they have no choice.
5) **Check Legality.**
	1) Check that all chosen targets are legal.
	2) Check that all costs were paid.
	3) Check that the outcome of the effect of this card being played would not create an illegal state.
		- *eg:* Check that a spell's execution does not create a state where a Battlefield has Units controlled by 3 different players.
		- If a [[Game Effect]] prevents the performance of a [[Game Action]], that effect doesn’t prevent cards and abilities that instruct a player to perform that game action from being played or finalized. On resolution, that game action will be skipped as it is an impossible instruction.
	4) Check that the card has the appropriate permissions to be played at this timing.
	5) If any of the above checks fail, the actions taken in this process are undone and the action is cancelled.
	6) Finish [[Finalize|finalizing]] this card and proceed with the card's category of Play.
