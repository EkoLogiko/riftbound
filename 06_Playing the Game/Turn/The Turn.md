Play continues cyclically in [[Turn Order]] until one player wins. The [[Turn Phases|phases]] of a turn are rigid, but the actions taken during those steps can be done in any order, unless otherwise specified.

[[Game Action|Game Actions]] of any nature are performed one at a time and are executed completely, and cannot be performed simultaneously for any reason.
If one or more [[Game Action|game actions]], [[Game Effect|game effects]], or [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] are activated simultaneously, then [[Turn Order]] is referenced to organize the sequence of actions, starting with the current [[Turn Player]].

When there are no items on the [[The Chain|Chain]] and the [[Turn Player]] cannot or chooses not to perform any [[Discretionary Action]], the current phase or step of the turn ends and the next phase, step, or turn begins.

At any given time, the turn is in one of four [[Turn States|states]].
