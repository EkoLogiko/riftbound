At any given time, up to one player has **Focus**: the permission to take appropriately timed [[Discretionary Action|Discretionary Actions]] when the turn is in a [[Turn States|Showdown Open State]].

A player who gains Focus also gains [[Priority]]. A player who passes [[Priority]] retains Focus.

A player may not make discretionary actions with Focus unless they also possess [[Priority]].

If the turn is in a [[Neutral State]], no player has Focus.
