1) [[Start of Turn]]
	1) [[Awaken Phase]]: All [[Permanent|permanents]] that can be readied become [[Ready]].
	2) [[Beginning Phase]]
		1) [[Beginning Step]]: *"At the start of the Beginning Phase"* [[Game Effect|game effects]] take place.
		2) [[Scoring Step]]: [[Hold|Holding]] happens.
	3) [[Channel Phase]]: [[Rune|Runes]] are [[Channel|channeled]].
	4) [[Draw Phase]]: [[Turn Player]] [[Draw|draws]].
2) [[Main Phase]]
	1) Empty [[Rune Pool]]
	2) *"At the start of the Main Phase"* [[Game Effect|game effects]] take place.
	3) The turn enters a [[Neutral State]] and an [[Open State]]. Turn Player may play cards and activate effects.
		1) [[Combat]] may occour.
		2) [[Showdown|Showdowns]] may occour.
3) [[Ending Phase]]
	1) [[Ending Step]]: *"At the end of the turn"* [[Game Effect|game effects]] take place.
	2) [[Expiration Step]]: Execute **Special [[Cleanup]]**:
		1) [[Heal]] all unit
		2) "This turn" effects expire
		3) Empty [[Rune Pool]]
	3) If [[FEPR]] happened, go back to 2.
