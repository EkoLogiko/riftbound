When all steps of the [[Start of Turn]] have been completed, the **Main Phase** begins.

At the *start of the Main Phase*, the following [[Task|Tasks]] become [[Outstanding Task|Outstanding]] in the specified order:
1) Each player's [[Rune Pool]] empties. Any unspent [[Energy]] and [[Power]] are lost.
2) "At the start of the Main Phase" [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] take place.

After the previous has been handled, the Main Phase has no defined structure:
- A player may take any number of [[Discretionary Action|Discretionary Actions]] they are able to perform during this phase.
- This is denoted as a [[Neutral State]] and [[Open State]], and only the Turn Player has the ability to play spells or activate abilities.
	- In a [[Mode of Play]] with teammates, the Turn Player's teammates may play spells and activate abilities, including ones without Action or Reaction. They can only do so when the Turn Player [[Invite|invites]] them to do so with their own [[Priority]].
- As a result of a player taking Discretionary Actions, one or more structured phases may occur.
	- See [[Combat]].
	- See [[Showdown]].

When a player has no more Discretionary Actions they wish to execute, they must indicate they are ending their turn. This ends the Main Phase and play proceeds to the [[Ending Phase]].
