At any given time, up to one player has **Priority**: the singular exclusive right to take [[Discretionary Action|Discretionary Actions]]. If no player has Priority, no player can take them.

Players can always take and make choices for [[Limited Action|Limited Actions]] when instructed, regardless of Priority.

A player receives Priority at the following times:
- When the [[The Turn|turn]] is in a [[Turn States|Neutral Open State]] during their [[Main Phase]].
- When the turn is in a [[Showdown State]] and they gain [[Focus]].
- When the turn is in a [[Closed State]], all [[Pending Item|pending chain items]] finish being [[Finalize|finalized]], and they control the next item on the [[The Chain|Chain]].
- When the turn is in a[[ Closed State]], they are the next Player in [[Turn Order]], and the player with Priority passes.

When a player is granted Priority, it is either created if no player has it or taken from the player with Priority.
