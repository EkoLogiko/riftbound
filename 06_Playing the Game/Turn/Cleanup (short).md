A short summary of [[Cleanup]].
1) Check [[Victory Score]].
2) ([[Combat]] only) Manage [[Attacker]] and [[Defender]].
	1) Add designations to [[Unit|Units]] without.
	2) Change designations to [[Unit|Units]] with the wrong one.
	3) Remove designations to [[Unit|Units]] at locations other than the battlefield where [[Combat]] is occouring.
3) Manage death and expiration step:
	1) Trigger [[Deathknell]] and *"When I die"*.
	2) Move killed [[Unit|Units]] to their respective [[Trash]].
	3) ([[Expiration Step]] only) [[Heal]] all [[Unit|Units]].
	4) ([[Expiration Step]] only) All "this turn" effects expire simultaneously
	5) ([[Expiration Step]] only) Each player's [[Rune Pool]] empties. Any unspent [[Energy]] and [[Power]] are lost.
4) ([[Open State]] only) Remove [[Control]] of [[Battlefield|Battlefields]].
5) Recall [[Permanent|Permanents]] which shouldn't be where they are.
6) Handle [[Showdown]] as staged.
7) Handle [[Combat]] as staged.
8) Remove or apply [[Contested Control|Contested]] status.
9) ([[Open State]] + [[Neutral State]] only) Start Staged [[Showdown|Showdowns]].
10) ([[Open State]] + [[Neutral State]] only) Start Staged [[Combat]].
11) ([[Showdown State]] + [[Neutral State]] only) Transform [[Non-Combat Showdown]] in [[Combat Showdown]].
