An **End of Turn Cleanup** is a special [[Cleanup]] occurring during the [[Expiration Step]] of the [[Ending Phase]].

This is a normal [[Cleanup]] which inserts at step 3:
- 3c) [[Heal]] all [[Unit|Units]]
- 3d) All "this turn" effects expire simultaneously
- 3e) Each player's [[Rune Pool]] empties. Any unspent [[Energy]] and [[Power]] are lost.
