**Delayed Replacements** are the [[Delayed Ability]] version of [[Replacement Effect|Replacement Effects]] that can be recognized by specifying the effect they are replacing at a specific time, or *“the [Nth] time”* in the description of the effect as it resolves.

Some **Delayed Replacements** take the form *“then recycle it,”* or *“then banish it,”* in reference to [[Chain Item|Chain Items]] being played by [[Linked Ability|Linked Abilities]] or [[Linked Instructions]]. These are short for *“if it would leave the chain after becoming a [[Finalized Chain Item]], and leaving the chain wasn’t instructed by its own execution, perform the specified game action instead.”*
