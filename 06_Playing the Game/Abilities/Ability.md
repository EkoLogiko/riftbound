**Abilities** are discrete pieces of text that describe what a card may or must do. They may appear on any card.
An **Ability** is the structured rules and capabilities of [[Game Object|Game Objects]] or [[Spell|Spells]].

Abilities can be played by following the [[Playing or Activating Abilities]] process.

A card can have more than one **Ability** and more than one *type of Ability*.
