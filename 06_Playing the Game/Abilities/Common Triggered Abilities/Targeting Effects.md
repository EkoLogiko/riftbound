**Targeting Effects** are [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] whose [[Trigger Condition]] includes a [[Game Object]] becoming [[Target|targeted]].

These are commonly structured as *“When you choose me …”* or *“When you choose a [Game Object] …”*

These [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] are put on [[The Chain]] as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] after a [[Spell|spell]] or [[Ability|ability]] that targets an appropriate [[Game Object]] is [[Finalize|Finalized]].

Although these abilities say *“choose”* in their [[Trigger Condition]], they trigger specifically when an appropriate [[Game Object]] is [[Target|Targeted]].
