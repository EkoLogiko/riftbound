**Play Effects** are [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] whose [[Trigger Condition]] includes the [[Permanent]] that has the Play Effect being played to [[The Board]].

These are commonly structured as *“When you play me…”* for [[Unit|Units]] and *“When you play this…”* for [[Gear]].

These [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] are put on [[The Chain]] as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] after the [[Permanent]] these effects correspond to is [[Finalize|Finalized]] and enters [[The Board]].

Abilities that trigger when another object is played **are not considered** Play Effects.
