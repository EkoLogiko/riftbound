**Attack Triggers** are [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] that trigger when a [[Unit]] or Player gains the [[Attacker]] designation **for the first time** during a [[Combat]].

These are commonly structured as *“When I attack…”* or *“When you attack…”*

These [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] are put on [[The Chain]] as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] after the [[Unit]] these effects correspond to gains the [[Attacker]] designation during [[Combat]].

These triggers will only have their [[Trigger Condition]] checked once per [[Combat]], despite a [[Unit]] being able to gain and lose the [[Attack]] designation multiple times in the same [[Combat]].

If the [[Trigger Condition]] contains other requirements besides attacking and if those requirements are not fulfilled when the unit gains the [[Attacker]] designation, it will not trigger in that [[Combat]].
