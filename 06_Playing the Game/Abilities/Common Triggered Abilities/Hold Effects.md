**Hold Effects** are [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] whose [[Trigger Condition]] includes a [[Unit]] being present at a [[Battlefield]] during the [[Beginning Phase]] when a player scores Victory Points from [[Hold|Holding]].

These are commonly structured as *“When I hold…”* or *“When you hold…”*

This category of [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] encompasses only those that are triggered from [[Unit|Units]] that were present during the [[Hold]] action, or [[Ability|Abilities]] that reference the player that performed the [[Hold]] action.

The **Hold Abilities** of [[Unit|Units]] are put on [[The Chain]] as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] after the [[Unit]] these effects correspond to are present at a [[Battlefield]] when a player maintains [[Control|control]] of it and Gains 1 Victory Point during their [[Beginning Phase]] from [[Hold|Holding]].

The **Hold Abilities** of anything that references the player [[Hold|Holding]] is put on [[The Chain]] as a [[Pending Chain Item]] when the [[Trigger Condition]] that the player that controls the triggering source has performed a [[Hold]] and gained 1 Victory Point.

The **Hold Abilities** of anything that references the player [[Hold|Holding]] is put on [[The Chain]] as a [[Pending Chain Item]] when the [[Trigger Condition]] that the player that controls the triggering source has performed a [[Hold]] and gained 1 Victory Point.

If the act of gaining one point from [[Hold|Holding]] is negated or replaced in any way, the Hold Effect **will still trigger**.
