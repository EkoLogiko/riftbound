**Conquer Effects** are [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] whose [[Trigger Condition]] includes a [[Unit]] participating in, and successfully [[Conquer|Conquering]] a [[Battlefield]].

These are commonly structured as *“When I conquer…”* and *“When you conquer…”*

This category of [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] encompasses only those that are triggered from [[Unit|Units]] that were present during the [[Conquer]] action, or [[Ability|Abilities]] that reference the player that performed the [[Conquer]] action.

The **Conquer Abilities** of *[[Unit|Units]]* are put on [[The Chain]] as [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] after the [[Unit]](s) these effects correspond to are present at a [[Battlefield]] when a player gains [[Control|control]] of it and gains 1 Victory Point from [[Conquer|Conquering]].

The **Conquer Abilities** of *anything that references* the player [[Conquer|Conquering]] is put on [[The Chain]] as a [[Pending Chain Item]] when the [[Trigger Condition]] that the player that controls the triggering source has performed a [[Conquer]] and gained 1 Victory Point.

If the act of gaining one point from [[Conquer|Conquering]] is negated or replaced in any way, the Conquer Effect **will still trigger**.
