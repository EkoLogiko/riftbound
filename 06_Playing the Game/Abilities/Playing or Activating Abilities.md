Playing or activating [[Ability|Abilities]] follows the same steps of the [[Playing a Card]] process.
Abilities when added to [[The Chain]] become [[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] until they complete the steps of [[Playing a Card]].
When an Ability finishes those steps it becomes a [[Finalized Chain Item]] just like a [[Spell]].
When an Ability with the [[Add]] action is [[Finalize|Finalized]] it resolves immediately, like a [[Unit]] or [[Gear]].
# The Process
1) **Activate or trigger the [[Ability]].**
	1) Add a [[Pending Chain Item]] to [[The Chain]] representing the [[Ability]] that is either being Activated or Triggered. Notably, although this [[Chain Item]] will not have a card representing it, this will create a [[Closed State]].
	2) If there is currently a [[Game Effect]] being resolved, continue resolving the game effect instead of continuing the following steps.
2) **Make relevant choices.**
	1) If the first part of a [[Triggered Ability]]’s effect is *“you may,”* or *“they may,”* its controller decides whether or not to perform the [[Triggered Ability]] now. If they choose not to perform the Triggered Ability now, it is removed from [[The Chain]].
	2) Make all choices required for this ability, such as targets, modes, or other relevant decisions. (as for [[Playing a Card]]).
	3) If legal options are not available for an [[Activated Abilities|Activated Ability]], it is not legal to activate it.
	4) If there are not enough options to make legal choices for a [[Triggered Ability]] that has been put on [[The Chain]], remove it from it now. It ceases to be a [[Pending Chain Item]] but never becomes a [[Finalized Chain Item]]. This is not an Ability being [[Countered]]. If there are legal options to choose, the ability’s controller must choose them. They may not decline this stage of playing a [[Triggered Ability]].
3) **Determine [[Total Cost]]**.
	1) Determine the [[Base Cost]] of the [[Ability]].
		- [[Activated Abilities]] will have a cost listed before the “:” in their text.
		- [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] will typically not have a [[Base Cost]] associated with them when placed on the chain due to their conditions. If a [[Triggered Ability]] has a [[Cost within Istructions]] (e.g. “[do X] to [do Y]”), the cost is taken as the [[Base Cost]].
	2) Apply [[Cost Increases]] and [[Cost Discounts]] as a result of choices made in the prior step.
	3) Apply any other [[Cost Increases]] or [[Cost Discounts]] as necessary.
4) **Pay [[Cost|Costs]]**
	1) Pay [[Cost|Costs]] as determined in the prior step.
	2) At this stage, players may decline to pay for [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] that have incurred a [[Cost]]. If they do, the ability will cease being a [[Pending Chain Item]] and be removed from [[The Chain]]. It never becomes a [[Finalized Chain Item]]. This is not an Ability being [[Countered]].
5) **[[Check Legality]]**
	1) Ensure that the [[Ability]]’s [[Target|targets]] are still legal.
	2) Ensure that the [[Ability]]’s effect would not create an illegal state. If it would, resolve in the same way you would resolve a Card that creates an illegal state.
6) **Proceed With Play**
	1) This [[Ability]] is no longer [[Pending Chain Item|Pending]] and becomes a [[Finalized Chain Item]].
	2) If there are other[[Pending Chain Item|Pending Chain Items]] on [[The Chain]], their controllers perform the remaining steps of playing now.
	3) Other players have an opportunity to play [[Reaction|Reactions]] before the resolution of spells and abilities.
	4) Otherwise, execute the [[Ability]] just like a [[Spell]], then clear the [[Chain Item]] from [[The Chain]].
