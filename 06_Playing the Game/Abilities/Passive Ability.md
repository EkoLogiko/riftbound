**Passive Abilities** are conditions, rules, constraints, or statements that affect the course of regular play.

These abilities have a wide variety of formats to recognize; in general, they can be recognized by being statements of fact.

>*Example*: "I get +1 [M] while you have 2 or more cards in your hand."
>*Example*: "Friendly Yordles at my battlefield have [[Shield]]."
# Passive Conditional Abilities
Passive Abilities can be *conditional*. They can be recognized by the occurrence of *"if"* or *"while"* as part of the statement of the ability.

>*Example*: "While I'm attacking or defending alone, I have +2 [M]."
>Example: "If an opponent controls a battlefield, I enter ready."
# Presence on Permanents
Passive Abilities of [[Permanent|Permanents]] are typically only active while on [[The Board]].
# Presence on Cards Outside the Board
Passive Abilities of cards in zones that are outside of [[The Board]] self-describe their context.

>*Example*: The passive ability **"Play me only during an opponent's turn."** applies in any zone from which that card can be played.
>*Example*: Undying Legion has a passive ability that reads *“[Legion][>] You may play me from your trash for [3][C].”* That passive ability only applies when Undying Legion is in the trash.

Passive Abilities can alter the [[Cost|Costs]] of cards as they are played. These apply at all times in any zone from which the card with the ability can be played.
