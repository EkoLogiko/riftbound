**Reflexive Triggers** are a type of [[Triggered Ability]] that create one or more [[Chain Item|Chain Items]] when their [[Trigger Condition]] is met.

**Reflexive Triggers** can be recognized by the phrase *“Do this:”* or *“Do one of the following:”*
- *“Do this”* can be followed by *“N times.”* The Reflexive Trigger will thus be added to [[The Chain]] N times when its [[Trigger Condition]] is met.
- *“Do one of the following”* can instead be *“Do X of the following”* or *“Do the following”*. The effect will specify which of the related Reflexive Triggers will go on [[The Chain]] when the [[Trigger Condition]] is met.

Reflexive Triggers will be preceded by their conditions, if any. If no [[Trigger Condition]] is present in the [[Ability]] then the Reflexive Trigger will always be added to [[The Chain]].

f present, the [[Trigger Condition]] of a Reflexive Trigger will follow the same format as a [[Triggered Ability]].
# Playing Reflexive Triggers
Reflexive Triggers use [[The Chain]].
A new ability is created and added to [[The Chain]] as a [[Pending Chain Item]].
If a Reflexive Trigger creates more than one [[Pending Chain Item]] it creates them all in order, but does not go beyond the first step of adding them to [[The Chain]].
