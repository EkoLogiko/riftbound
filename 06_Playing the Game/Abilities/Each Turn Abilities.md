Some [[Replacement Effect|Replacement Effects]], [[Passive Ability|Passive Abilities]] and [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] will begin with:
- *Once each turn*
- *N times each turn*
- *Once on each of your turns*
- *N times on each of your turns*
# Replacement Effects and Triggered Abilities
Such [[Replacement Effect|Replacement Effects]] and [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] will only be applied to the specified number of events each turn, or performed the specified number of times each turn. Once they have been performed that many times or been applied to that many events, they cannot trigger or be applied to a later event in the same turn.

If such [[Triggered Ability]] or [[Replacement Effect]] is optional, the controller of the ability has the choice of whether or not to perform or apply it.
- For optional [[Replacement Effect|Replacement Effects]], when an event the [[Replacement Effect]] could apply to occurs, the player who controls the [[Replacement Effect]] may choose not to apply it to the event. In that case, it has not been applied this turn.
- For optional [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]], during [[Finalize|finalization]] of the [[Triggered Ability]], the player who controls the [[Triggered Ability]] may choose not to perform it. In that case, it is removed from [[The Chain]].
>*Example*: Zilean, Time mage reads “Once each turn, if you would play a token unit while I'm at a battlefield, you may play that token and an additional copy of it instead.” When his controller plays a token, they can choose not to apply the replacement effect to that event. If they do, they can choose to apply it to a later event of a token being played.
>*Example:* A player controls a unit that reads in part “Once each turn, when an enemy unit dies, you may banish it.” When an enemy Recruit token dies, the triggered ability goes on the chain. If they choose not to perform the ability on finalization, it is removed from the chain. When a Stalwart Poro dies later in the turn, they can choose to perform it then.
# Passive Abilties
For such [[Passive Ability|Passive Abilities]], they will be active until they have been applied to the specified number of events, after which point they will become inactive.
If such a [[Passive Ability]] says *“you may”* or *“they may”* as the first part of the ability after the *“once each turn,”* the controller of the source of the [[Passive Ability]] chooses whether or not to apply the ability to the event in question. If they do not, the [[Passive Ability]] is *Ignored* during that event.
