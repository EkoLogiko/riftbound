The combat statistic of a Unit. Used to determine a Unit's contribution to [[Combat]], as well as when it is [[Kill|Killed]] by damaging effects.

A [[Unit]]'s **Might** can be referenced by cards and effects in one of three forms:
- **Printed Might**: the value in the top right corner of the card/token.
- **Base Might**: Trait that is alterable by effects in the [[Trait-Altering Effects]] [[Layer]].
- **Effective Might**: Value calculated in the [[Arithmetic Effects]] [[Layer]] including all Might altering [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
Card text may refer to the latter two of these three forms. When card or these rules refer to a [[Unit]]'s **Effective Might**, they will do so by referring to that unit's "Might".
>*Example*: A card reads in part “Choose a unit. Its Base Might becomes 5 this turn.” This applies an effect in the trait-altering layer.
>*Example*: A card reads in part “Choose a unit. Increase its Might to 5 this turn.” This applies an effect in the arithmetic layer.

If a Unit ever has nonzero damage marked on it equalling or exceeding its **Might**, it is [[Kill|Killed]].

If a unit's Might is ever less than 0, it is treated as 0 when referenced by spells and abilities, and when summing Might to be assigned as damage in the [[Combat Damage Step]].

Although the unit’s Might is treated as 0, it is not 0. Effects that calculate Might increases and decreases use the actual value of the unit’s Might.
