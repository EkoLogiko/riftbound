A card can have one or more **Categories** and **Sub-Categories** based on the properties of its front and back sides. They dictate the behaviors of the card during play.

[[Spell|Spells]] and other effects can refer to categories, sub-categories, [[Supertype|supertypes]], [[Card Type|card types]], [[Tag|Tags]], and other characteristics inclusively or exclusively.
When characteristics are reffered to in this way, it refers to any [[Game Object]] with the appropriate characteristic on [[The Board]], unless the [[Zone]] is specified implicitly or explicitly by the effect doing the referring or the nature of the characteristic.

> *Example*: A "non-unit card" is any card that is not a unit.
> *Example*: A "unit" is any game object that is a unit, regardless of any other categories it belongs to.
> *Example*: A "Yeti" refers to a game object with the Yeti tag on the board.
> *Example*: A "spell" refers to a spell on the chain, because spells cannot exist as game objects on the board.
> *Example*: A "spell in your trash" refers to a spell card in your trash because the effect specifies the zone in question.
