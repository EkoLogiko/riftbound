**Champion Legends** are [[Game Object|Game Objects]]. They are played in the [[Legend Zone]] at the start of the game and cannot typically leave it during play.
They are not [[Permanent|Permanents]] and may have one or more [[Domain|Domains]], which dictate the [[Domain Identity]] of the deck.

Legends are [[Ownership|Owned]] by a player, which [[Control|Controls]] them.
Legends may be [[Create|Created]] during the course of play. They will be [[Create|Created]] [[Ready]] unless the effect specifies otherwise.

Legends **cannot** be:
- [[Kill|Killed]] during the course of regular play
- [[Move|Moved]]

Legends **can**:
- be [[Target|targeted]] by [[Spell|spells]] or [[Game Effect|game effects]]
