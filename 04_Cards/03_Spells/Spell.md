**Spell** is a [[Card Type]] of [[Main Deck Card]]s.
A spell can be played during an [[Open State]] outside of [[Showdown]]s on its controller's turn. A spell is controlled by the player who played it.

A spell creates a [[Game Effect]] according to its [[Instructions]] and is then placed in the [[Trash]] of the player who owns it. When a spell is successfully played, a player executes the [[Rules Text]] of the spell. This is called [[Spell Resolution|resolving]] the spell.

Certain [[Keyword]]s on spells are not executable [[Rules Text]], but instead intrinsic properties of the spell. These determine inherent properties and behaviors of the spell before being played or while on the chain.
