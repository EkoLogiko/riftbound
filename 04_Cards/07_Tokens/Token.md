**Tokens** are [[Game Object|Game Objects]] created by [[Spell|spells]] and [[Ability|abilities]] during play. They can be represented by anything. Printed tokens are included in Riftbound booster packs, but they are not required to play a token.

The effect that creates a token may specify the conditions or circumstances under which it enters the board. These stipulations may alter the usual steps for [[Playing a Card|playing a card]] if the token is played:
- The effect may state that the token enters [[Ready|ready]] or [[Exhaust|exhausted]], if that state is contrary to the default for the token's type.
- The effect may restrict the [[Location|location]] to which the token may be played.
- The effect may grant temporary abilities or modifications to the token.

Tokens are [[Create|Created]] on the board or the [[The Chain|Chain]] and cannot exist elsewhere. If a token is put into any [[Non-Board Zone]] besides the chain, it ceases to exist immediately after moving to its new zone.

The spell or ability that Creates a token specifies some of its characteristics.
# Tokens and Cards
Tokens are **NOT** cards. It is an instrinsic category of [[Game Object|Game Objects]].
A token cannot lose its token nature by any means and cards cannot become tokens by any means.
## Common Properties
They still have some properties in common with cards:
- They can be [[Playing a Card|played]] by if their card type is played, following all the applicable steps for playing a card plus any restrictions or modifications from the effect that created the token.
- Token *units* have [[Might]].
- Tokens may have one or more [[Tag]].
- Tokens have a [[Card Type|type]]. They follow all rules for their type unless otherwise specified.
- Tokens inherit the [[Recycle]] destination of their type.

>*Example:* A token unit is a unit. It enters exhausted, can take the standard move action, deals damage equal to its Might in combat, is destroyed if it takes damage equal to or greater than its might, can be chosen or otherwise affected by spells or abilities that choose or affect units, etc.

>*Example:* Token runes will be recycled to the Rune Deck. Token units, gear, and spells will be recycled to the Main Deck. Token battlefields and legends can’t be recycled.
## Different Properties
They differ from cards in some ways:
- Tokens do not have [[Cost|costs]], but their cost is treated as being 0 for all purposes. Tokens can have costs appended to them via applied [[Layer]] effects.
- Tokens do not have [[Domain|Domains]], but the can have domains appended to them via applied [[Layer]] effects.

# Control and Ownership
A token's [[Control|controller]] is the controller of the spell or ability that created it, unless the token's type innately determines control or that spell or ability specifies that a different player is the token's controller.
A token's [[Ownership|owner]] is the player who controlled the effect that created it.
# List of Known Tokens
- A 1 [M] Recruit token is a domainless unit token with 1 Might and the Recruit tag.
- A 3 [M] Sprite token with Temporary is a domainless unit token with 3 Might, the Fae tag, and the Temporary keyword. See rule 816. Temporary for more information.
- A 2 [M] Sand Soldier token is a domainless unit token with 2 Might and the Shurima tag.
- A 3 [M] Mech token is a domainless unit token with 3 Might and the Mech tag. 
- A Gold gear token is a domainless gear token with “[Reaction][>] Kill this, [E]: [Add] [A].”
- A 0 [M] Reflection token is a domainless unit token with 0 Might.
- A 1 [M] Bird token is a domainless unit token with 1 Might, the Bird tag, and the Deflect keyword. See [[Deflect]] for more information.
- A Brush battlefield token is a domainless battlefield token with “Bird, Cat, Dog, Poro, and Ivern units here have +1 [M]” and “When you score here, you may replace this with the battlefield it replaced.”
- The Baron Pit battlefield token is a domainless battlefield token with “Units can move here from anywhere.”
- A 1 [M] Tentacle token is a domainless unit token with 1 Might and the Bilgewater tag.
- A 0 [M] Shadow Clone token is a domainless unit token with 0 Might and “When I attack, you may banish a unit from your trash. If you do, give me Assault 4 this turn.” See [[Assault]] for more information.
