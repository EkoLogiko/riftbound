**Tokens** are [[Game Object|Game Objects]] created by [[Spell|spells]] and [[Ability|abilities]] during play. They can be represented by anything. Printed tokens are included in Riftbound booster packs, but they are not required to play a token.

The effect that creates a token may specify the conditions or circumstances under which it enters the board. These stipulations may alter the usual steps for [[Playing a Card|playing a card]] if the token is played:
- The effect may state that the token enters [[Ready|ready]] or [[Exhaust|exhausted]], if that state is contrary to the default for the token's type.
- The effect may restrict the [[Location|location]] to which the token may be played.
- The effect may grant temporary abilities or modifications to the token.
# Control and Ownership
A token's [[Control|controller]] is the controller of the spell or ability that created it, unless the token's type innately determines control or that spell or ability specifies that a different player is the token's controller.
A token's [[Ownership|owner]] is the player who controlled the effect that created it.

- A 1 [M] Recruit token is a domainless unit token with 1 Might and the Recruit tag.
- A 3 [M] Sprite token with Temporary is a domainless unit token with 3 Might, the Fae tag, and the Temporary keyword. See rule 816. Temporary for more information.
- A 2 [M] Sand Soldier token is a domainless unit token with 2 Might and the Shurima tag.
- A 3 [M] Mech token is a domainless unit token with 3 Might and the Mech tag.
