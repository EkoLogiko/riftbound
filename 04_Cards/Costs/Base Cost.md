These [[Cost|costs]] are the most fundamental costs that must be paid to finalize a card or [[Ability]].
It appears on cards in the upper left corner of the card.
# On [[Activated Abilities]]
The Base Cost is the resource or instruction written before the “:”. If there are multiple choices for costs to pay for an [[Activated Abilities|Activated Ability]] before the ":", the **Base Cost** is whatever cost the player chooses from among those options.

>*Example*: Legion Marauder is a unit that reads in part “[Empower] — [1] or [C].” This is functionally short for “[1] or [C]: Empower me. Play only if not empowered.” When finalizing the ability to the chain, the controller of the chain item will choose one of the base costs to pay. That is the base cost of the ability.
# On [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]]
The Base Cost is present in the [[Cost within Istructions]] as the first part of the effect. It must be paid to [[Finalize]] the [[Triggered Ability]].

>*Example*: Overzealous Fan is a unit that reads “When I defend, you may kill me to move an attacking unit to its base.” In order to finalize the ability to the chain, its controller must kill Overzealous Fan.
# Base Cost Modifications
- If an ability or instruction allows you to play a card “for [Cost]”, replace the card’s Base Costs with [Cost].
- If an ability or instruction allows you to *"ignore"* one or more of a card's costs, set the appropriate [[Base Cost]](s) of the card to zero.
	- If a card allows a player to play a card "ignoring its cost," its base [[Energy]] cost and base [[Power]] cost are set to zero.
	- If a card instructs a player to play a card "ignoring its [[Energy]] cost" or "ignoring its [[Power]] cost," only the appropriate cost is set to zero, and the remaining cost still applies.
	- Further additional costs and/or cost increases applied in subsequent steps may raise the card's [[Total Cost]] above zero.
- Effects that refer to the [[Base Cost]] of the card refer to the Printed or [[07_Additional Rules/Copy|Copied]] [[Base Cost]] of said card, and not the modified [[Base Cost]] that is used when determining the [[Total Cost]] of the card.
