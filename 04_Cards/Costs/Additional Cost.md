These [[Cost|costs]] are in addition to the base cost of the [[Spell]] or [[Ability]] and will include *“as an additional cost.”*
Additional Costs must be paid to finalize the spell or ability, in addition to the [[Base Cost]].

**Additional Costs** can be:
- **Mandatory** --> These costs must be paid to complete playing the card. They use the phrase *"as an additional cost"* and don't include the word *"may."*
	- The cost imposed by the [[Deflect]] keyword is a Mandatory Additional Cost.
- **Optional** --> These costs must be paid only if the player made the choice to pay them in step 2 of the [[Playing a Card]] process. They use the phrase *"as an additional cost"* and the word *"may."*

>*Example*: A unit has the passive ability **"As an additional cost to play me, kill a friendly unit."** This is a **Mandatory Additional Cost**.
>*Example*: A unit has the ability *"As you play me, you may discard 1 as an additional cost. If you do, reduce my cost by [2]."* This is an **Optional Additional Cost**.
