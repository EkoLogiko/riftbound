**Total Cost** is the total [[Cost]] to be paid in order to [[Playing a Card|Play a Card]].
It is calculated during the process of [[Playing a Card]] and is the sum of the [[Base Cost]] plus every [[Additional Cost]], costs increases and cost decreases.

If an effect allows a player to play a card ignoring “any and all costs,” set the total cost to [0], including any non-standard costs.

[[Energy]] and [[Power]] costs can't be reduced below 0.
