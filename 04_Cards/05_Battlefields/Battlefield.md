Battlefields are [[Game Object]]s which start the game on [[The Board]] and remain in place for the duration of regular play. They are not [[Permanent|permanents]].

Battlefields are [[Ownership|Owned]] by a player.
Battlefield cannot be [[Move|Moved]].

A **Battlefield** is a [[Location]] that can be the [[Origin]] or [[Destination]] of moves.

Any number of [[Unit]]s can be present at a Battlefield.
**Battlefields** and *[[permanent]]s at Battlefields* are [[Public Information]].

Battlefields can be [[Target|targeted]] by [[Spell]]s or [[Game Effect]]s.

# Battlefield States
- *occupied*: There is at least one [[Unit]] present at the Battlefield.
- *uncontrolled*: No player controls the Battlefield.
- *open*: The Battlefield is both unoccupied and uncontrolled.
