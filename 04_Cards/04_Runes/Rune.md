**Rune** is a [[Card Type]] and is **NOT** a [[Main Deck Card]], thus is not a [[Permanent]] either. They reside in the [[Rune Deck]].
Runes produce the resources needed to pay costs. They produce both [[Energy]] and [[Power]].

# Basic Runes
There are six **Basic Runes**, each with a [[Domain]] corresponding to its name:
- Fury Rune
- Calm Rune
- Mind Rune
- Body Rune
- Chaos Rune
- Order Rune
A Basic Rune always has the follong two [[Ability]]:
- [E]: [[Reaction|[Reaction]]] - [[Add]] [1]
- [[Recycle]] this: [[Reaction|[Reaction]]] - [[Add]] [C]
	- The [[Power]] added this way corresponds to the [[Domain]] of the Rune that is being Recycled.
