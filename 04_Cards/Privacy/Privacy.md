A card will always exist in one of the many [[Zone]]s described during [[Setup Process]].
A card will have a different level of **Privacy** based on its state or where it resides at a given time. Unless specified otherwise by the state of the card, a card’s Privacy is the same as the zone it is located in.

# Privacy Levels
- [[Secret Information]]
- [[Private Information]]
- [[Public Information]]

A player cannot be compelled to perform an action on cards whose privacy is secret or private if that action specifies a type or quality of card. If an effect would compel a player to perform an action on cards whose privacy is secret or private, and if that effect specifies a type or quality of card, the player in question may choose to ignore that instruction. Ignored instructions are deemed impossible.

> *Example:* A card effect reads, “Play a unit from your hand, ignoring its costs.” Although the effect does not say “may”, the player can choose not to perform the action even if they have a unit in their hand, because the effect specifies a card type.
