**Gear** are [[Game Object]]s while:
- On [[The Board]]
	- Can only be played to a player's [[Base]] unless an effect specifies otherwise.
	- Gear and their details are [[Public Information]] while on the Board.
	- Gear can be chosen, affected, or manipulated by spells, effects, or game actions that specify *Gear*.
	- Gear can be [[Kill]]ed.
- In a [[Trash]]
	- Are treated as Cards before Game Objects, similar to the [[Hand]].
	- They retain the properties of being Gear, but are not on the Board and thus cannot take actions or be affected by spells, abilities, or [[Game Action]]s that affect *Gear* on the Board.
	- Gear can be affected by spells and game effects that affect Gear in the Trash.

Gear is also a [[Card Type]].

**Gear** can have the [[Equipment]] tag

Gear may have [[Activated Abilities]].
- The Activated Ability of Gear may be executed at any time during the controlling player's [[Main Phase]] during an [[Open State]], and not during a [[Showdown]].
- This follows the same process as [[Playing a Card]]. This behaves, once activated, like a spell without an associated card.
# Intrinsic Properties
Gear have multiple [[Intrinsic Properties]] unique to them:
- Gear enter play [[Ready]].
- If an unattached non-Unit Gear is at a [[Battlefield]] for any reason during a [[Cleanup]], then it is [[Recall]]ed to its controller's [[Base]] as a corrective action.
- Gear have a [[Location]] which is the [[Base]] or [[Battlefield]] it currently occupies. 

>*Example: Gear are played to Base unless an effect specifies otherwise.* A gear played from [[Facedown]], which is specified to be played to the Battlefield it was played from, will enter at that Battlefield. Gear can also become located at a Battlefield if they are attached to a unit who becomes located at a Battlefield. A Gear that is a Unit can move to a Battlefield.
