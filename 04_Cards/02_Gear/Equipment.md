A [[Gear]] can have the *Equipment* tag. Those Gear are referred to as **Equipment**.

Equipment have [[Effect Text]] and a [[Might Bonus]].
Equipment can have the [[Equip]] or [[Quick-Draw]] [[Keyword]]s.

Equipment are still [[Gear]] and have the same intrinsic properties and rules that Gear do.
Equipment and whether a Gear has the *Equipment* tag is a characteristic of the Gear may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect]]s.
