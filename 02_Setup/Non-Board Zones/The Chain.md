The Chain is a [[Non-Board Zone]] that temporarily exists whenever a card is player or an ability is activated. Cards and abilities are placed here as part of the process of being played. The Chain exists as long as at least one [[Chain Item]] is on it.

Only one Chain can exist at a time. If a card or token would begin to be played while a Chain already exists, it is placed on the existing Chain.

The [[Turn States|State of Turn]] is partially determined by whether or not the Chain currently exists. (see [[Open State]] and [[Closed State]])

Cards, tokens, and abilities added to the chain are added as [[Pending Chain Item]] that become [[Finalized Chain Item]].

Cards and abilities on the **Chain** are [[Public Information]].
