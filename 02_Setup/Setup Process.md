1) Each player separates their [[Champion Legend]] and places it in the [[Legend Zone]].
2) Each player separates their [[Chosen Champion]] and places it in the [[Champion Zone]].
3) Each player sets aside their [[Battlefield]]s. Your [[Mode of Play]] will dictate how these are used and placed into the [[Battlefield Zone]].
4) Each player shuffles their [[Main Deck]]s and [[Rune Deck]]s, separately, then places them into their respective Zones ([[Main Deck Zone]] and [[Rune Deck Zone]]).
5) Determine [[Turn Order]] using any fair random method agreed on by all players.
6) Players each draw 4.
7) In turn order, players perform their [[Mulligan]].
8) Begin play with the [[First Player]] taking their turn.
