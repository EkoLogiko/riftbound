The space for each player's [[Champion Legend]], which is here a [[Game Object]].
The Champion Legend cannot be removed, moved, or displaced from this zone. Some effects may add a legend card or token to the **Legend Zone**. These legends are not Champion Legends.
Non-Champion legends may be removed from the **Legend Zone**. They cannot exist in any zone other than the **Legend Zone** or [[Banishment]].

This is not a location.
