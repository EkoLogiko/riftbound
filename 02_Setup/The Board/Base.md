Each player has their own **Base**.
Each Base is a [[Location]].
[[Permanent|Permanents]] (including those [[Attached]] to another permanent, even if not of the same controller) and [[Rune|Runes]] controlled by a player reside in that player's Base.

[[Permanent|Permanents]] and [[Rune|Runes]] in **Bases** are [[Public Information]].
