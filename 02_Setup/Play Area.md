The **Play Area** is a collection of logical [[Zone]]s that are defined by their behaviors and relationships to [[Player]]s. These Zones are grouped into [[The Board]] and [[Non-Board Zone]].

All [[Game Object]]s in the collective **Play Areas** are [[Public Information]]. Any player may view, or be provided with the details of the face-up information printed on the cards in the Play Area.
The [[State]] of all [[Game Object]]s in the **Play Area** is also [[Public Information]].
