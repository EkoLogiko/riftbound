[[Keyword]] for certain [[Spell]]s which grants all cases and rules of [[Action]].
In addition to all prior cases, may also be played during all forms of [[Closed State]]. This will result in this spell [[Spell Resolution|resolving]] before spells and abilities that are already on the [[The Chain|chain]].
