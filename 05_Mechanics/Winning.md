Players win Riftbound games primarily through gaining points.
Players can gain points a number of ways:
- [[Hold|Holding]] a [[Battlefield]].
- [[Conquer]] a [[Battlefield]].
- [[Spell|Spells]], [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] and [[Activated Abilities]] that instruct them to gain one or more points.
- When an opponent [[Burning Out|Burns Out]] and picks that player to gain 1 point.

**A player wins the game** if, in a [[Cleanup]], they have points greater than or equal to the [[Victory Score]] and more points than any other player. If more than one player has points greater than or equal to the Victory Score, whichever player has more points wins. If those players have the same number of points, play continues until one player has more points in a cleanup.

Players cannot have less than 0 points. If a player would lose 1 or more points while they have 0 points, nothing occurs and any effects that trigger on a player losing points do not trigger.

A player also wins the game if an effect instructs them to do so, or if they are the only player remaining in the game.

If an effect instructs a player to lose the game, that player is immediately removed from the game (see [[Conceding]]).

When a player wins the game, the game ends.
