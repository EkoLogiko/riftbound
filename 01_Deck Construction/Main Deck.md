The **Main Deck** has at least 40 [[Main Deck Card|Main Deck Cards]]:
- 1 [[Chosen Champion]] Unit
- [[Unit|Units]]
- [[Gear]]
- [[Spell|Spells]]
Your Main Deck can include up to 3 copies of the same named card. This includes your [[Chosen Champion]].
Cards have different names even if they represent the same character.

Subject to [[Domain Identity]].
Subject to card legality of the Format being played.

Your deck may only contain 3 total [[Signature Card]]s that have the same [[Champion]] tag as your [[Champion Legend]].
## Privacy
The number of the cards in a player's **Main Deck** is [[Public Information]].
The order of cards in the **Main Deck** is [[Secret Information]] during the course of play.
