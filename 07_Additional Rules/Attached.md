**Attached** is the state of a card being linked to another card in this way. A card remains in this state until **Detached**.

While in this state, the card’s printed [[Rules Text]] is [[Inactive]]; [[Ability|Abilities]] in the card’s [[Effect Text]] are appended to the [[Rules Text]] of the [[Top-Most Card]]. Moreover, the card’s [[Might Bonus]] modulates the [[Top-Most Card]]’s [[Might]] by the value listed.

**Attached** cards still have all properties of being a card on [[The Board]] while in this state, so they still have all [[Card Type|Types]] and [[Tag|Tags]] and still can be chosen or [[Target|targeted]] by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] while **Attached**.

**Attached** cards cannot be [[Move|moved]] separately from the [[Top-Most Card]] they are Attached to.

A card may be Attached **only to a single [[Top-Most Card]]** at a time.

Attached cards **may have different [[Control|Controllers]]** from their [[Top-Most Card]]. Changes in [[Control]] of the [[Top-Most Card]] **do not** impact [[Control]] of **Attached** cards and vice versa. An Attached card still appends the [[Ability|Abilities]] in its [[Effect Text]] to the [[Rules Text]] of the [[Top-Most Card]] and modulates the [[Top-Most Card]]’s [[Might]] by its [[Might Bonus]].
