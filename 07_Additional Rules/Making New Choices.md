Some [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] will instruct a player to **make new choices** for a [[Finalized Chain Item|Finalized Item]] on [[The Chain]]. **Making new choices** in this way requires a player to choose [[Game Object]], players, zones, or modes that were previously not being chosen by the [[Chain Item]].

This refers to the choices normally made during [[Finalize|finalization]], with some exclusions:
- The relevant choices that can be remade for these [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] are [[Location|Locations]] to be played to, modes, [[Destination|Destinations]], and [[Target|Targets]].
- This **does not** refer to any choices made *“as you play this”* or *“as I am played,”* or any choices made for *Optional* [[Additional Cost|Additional Costs]].

When **making new choices** in this way, a player must choose to remake all choices made for a [[Chain Item]], even if they choose to make the same choice that was previously made. A player may not choose to **make new choices** for a [[Chain Item]] if there aren’t legal choices that they could make in this way. In such case, the instruction to **make new choices** is negated.

Restrictions and requirements ([[Illegal Choices, Mandatory Requirements and Restrictions]]) applied to a [[Chain Item]] will also apply to **new choices made** for that [[Chain Item]] in this way.
>*Example*: If a card is played from facedown, it gains the restriction that it can only target game objects at the battlefield it was played from unless its targeting is restricted in such a way that choosing a unit at that battlefield is impossible. If a player makes new choices for such a card, they must still choose from among objects at that battlefield.

If, as a result of new choices, the [[Chain Item]] [[Target|targets]] a [[Game Object]], even if it previously [[Target|targeted]] the same [[Game Object]], any Targeting Effects of that [[Game Object]] will [[Triggered Ability|trigger]] as appropriate.

Any [[Cost|Costs]] *“to play”* the [[Chain Item]] that are incurred as a result of **new choices made** in this way are ignored. *The spell or ability is already played and its costs paid*. Any further costs incurred have no effect on the [[Finalized Chain Item]].
