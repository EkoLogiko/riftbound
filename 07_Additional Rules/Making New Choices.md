Some [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] will instruct a player to **make new choices** for a [[Finalized Chain Item|Finalized Item]] on [[The Chain]]. **Making new choices** in this way requires a player to choose [[Game Object]], players, zones, or modes that were previously not being chosen by the [[Chain Item]].

This refers to the choices normally made during [[Finalize|finalization]], with some exclusions:
- The relevant choices that can be remade for these [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] are [[Location|Locations]] to be played to, modes, [[Destination|Destinations]], and [[Target|Targets]].
- This **does not** refer to any choices made *“as you play this”* or *“as I am played,”* or any choices made for *Optional* [[Additional Cost|Additional Costs]].

When **making new choices** in this way, a player can choose any subset of the possible choices to remake, so long as doing so doesn’t lead to an illegal state, even if there is no other options. A player may not choose to **make new choices** for a [[Spell]] or [[Ability]] if there aren’t legal choices that they could make in this way.

If, as a result of new choices, the [[Spell]] or [[Ability]] [[Target|targets]] a [[Game Object]] it previously did not [[Target|target]], any Targeting Effects of that [[Game Object]] will [[Triggered Ability|trigger]] as appropriate at that time.

Any [[Cost|Costs]] *“to play”* the [[Spell]] or [[Ability]] that are incurred as a result of **new choices made** in this way are ignored. *The spell or ability is already played and its costs paid*. Any further costs incurred have no effect on the [[Finalized Chain Item]].
