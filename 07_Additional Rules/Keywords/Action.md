**Action** is a *Permissive* [[Keyword]]. It is present on Cards, [[Rune]] [[Ability|Abilities]], [[Champion Legend|Legend]] [[Ability|Abilities]] or [[Permanent]] [[Ability|Abilities]]. **Action** grants the corresponding card or effect permission to be [[Playing a Card|played]] or [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activated]] during [[Showdown]], even when it is not the [[Control|controlling]] player's turn.

**Action** is functionally short for the following:
- *On Cards* --> **"This can be played during [[Showdown|Showdowns]] on any player's turn."**
- *On Activated Abilities* --> **"This can be activated during [[Showdown|Showdowns]] on any player's turn."**

**Action** is formatted as “[Action]” on spells, or “[Action] [>]” on abilities.

The card or effect with this [[Keyword]] **is not restricted** to [[Showdown]]. This permission is inclusive of all other timings and options available to the ability as written or by default.

**Action** does not alter the function of any instruction of the corresponding card or effect it is on. It is only permission.

>*Example*: Playing a Unit with Action still has the inherent restrictions of playing Units without Action. It can only be played to the controlling player's base or a battlefield they control.

Some [[Passive Ability|Passive Abilities]] may grant a card or ability **Action** under certain conditions. The card or ability does not have the **Action** [[Keyword]] unless and until those circumstances are true.
Those conditions might only be fulfilled while the card or ability is on [[The Chain]]. In such a case, it can still be [[Playing a Card|played]] or [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activated]] at the appropriate timing as long as doing so could fulfill the conditions. If the [[Chain Item]] does not fulfill the conditions by the time step 5: check legality has been reached, the actions taken while playing it are undone and it is returned to the zone it was played from if it is a card.

**Action** is a referenceable characteristic:
- Whether or not a [[Game Object]] has **Action** is a characteristic of that [[Game Object]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
- Whether or not a [[Spell]] has **Action** is a characteristic of that [[Spell]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
- Whether or not an [[Ability]] has **Action** is a characteristic of that [[Ability]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
