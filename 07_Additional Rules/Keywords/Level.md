**Level** is a [[Dependent Keyword]].

It is formatted as **"[Level [N] [>] [Text]"**.

Starting from the [[Keyword]] to the end of the clause, the entire statement is the *Level [[Ability]]*.

It is functionally short for **“While you have [N] or more [[XP]], this card gains ‘[Text]’.”**
The [Text] here is the [[Dependent Ability]].

As long as the [[Control|controlling]] player has [N] [[XP]], then the [[Dependent Ability]] will be be [[Active]] on the card with **Level**. If the [[Control|controller]] of the card with **Level** changes, then the [[Dependent Ability]] will be rendered [[Active]] or [[Inactive]] based on the new controller’s [[XP]]. The [[Dependent Ability]] will be [[Inactive]] as soon as the controlling player has less than [N] [[XP]].

**Level**, and whether or not a card has **Level** is a characteristic of the card and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
