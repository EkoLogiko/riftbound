**Unique** is a *Deck Constraint Permission* [[Keyword]]. It is normally present on [[Main Deck Card|Main Deck Cards]].

**Unique** is not functionally short for any [[Rules Text]], and instead provides a restriction to players during [[Deck Building]].

A deck can contain only **one card of a given name** if the card has **Unique**.

If a card is a [[Signature]] card and is also **Unique**, then that deck can contain any combination of three [[Signature]] cards, but still only one of each named **Unique** card.

Cards with **Unique** have no additional effects during gameplay.

**Unique**, and whether or not a Card has **Unique**, is a characteristic of the card and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
