**Reaction** is a *Permissive* [[Keyword]]. It can be present on Cards, [[Rune]] [[Ability|Abilities]], [[Champion Legend|Legend]] [[Ability|Abilities]] and [[Permanent]] [[Ability|Abilities]].

**Reaction** grants the corresponding card or effect all abilities and permissions of [[Action]]. Additionally, it is functionally short for the following:
- **On Cards** --> "This can be [[Playing a Card|played]] during a [[Closed State]] on any player's turn."
- **On Activated Abilities** --> "This can be [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activated]] during a [[Closed State]] on any player's turn."
- 
Reaction is formatted as **“[Reaction]”** on cards, or **“[Reaction] [>]”** on abilities.

The corresponding card or effect with this [[Keyword]] is not restricted to a [[Closed State]] or [[Showdown|Showdowns]]. This permission is inclusive of all other timings and options available to the ability as written, [[Action]]'s permissions, or by default.

Reaction does not alter the function of any instruction of the Card, [[Rune]], or Effect it is on. *It is only Permission.* Playing [[Unit|Units]] with **Reaction** still has the inherent restrictions of playing [[Unit|Units]] without **Reaction**. It can only be played to the controlling player's [[Base]] or a [[Battlefield]] they [[Control|control]].

Some [[Passive Ability]] may grant a card or ability **Reaction** under certain conditions. The card or ability does not have the **Reaction** [[Keyword]] unless and until those circumstances are true.
Those conditions might only be fulfilled while the card or ability is on [[The Chain]]. In such a case, it can still be played or activated at the appropriate timing as long as doing so could fulfill the conditions. If the [[Chain Item]] does not fulfill the conditions by the time step 5: check legality has been reached, the actions taken while playing it are undone and it is returned to the zone it was played from if it is a card.

**Reaction** is a referencable characteristic:
- Whether or not a [[Game Object]] has **Reaction** is a characteristic of that [[Game Object]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
- Whether or not a [[Spell]] has **Reaction** is a characteristic of that [[Spell]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
- Whether or not a [[Ability]] has **Reaction** is a characteristic of that [[Ability]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
