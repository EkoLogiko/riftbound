**Accelerate** is a [[Keyword]] which represents an [[Unit]] [[Ability|ability]] and is functionally short for **"As you play me, you may pay [1] [C] as an [[Additional Cost]]. If you do, I enter [[Ready|ready]]."**

If the [[Unit|unit]] has one or more [[Domain|Domains]], the [[Power]] portion of the **Accelerate** [[Cost|cost]] can be paid only with a [[Power]] that matches one of the [[Domain|Domains]] of the [[Unit|unit]].
If the [[Unit|unit]] has no [[Domain]], the [[Power]] portion of the **Accelerate** [[Cost|cost]] can be paid with [A] (a [[Power of Any Domain]]).

**Accelerate** is an *Optional* [[Additional Cost]] to be paid as a player plays the [[Unit|unit]] with the [[Ability|ability]]. **Accelerate** costs cannot be paid while the unit is on [[The Board]], only as part of the steps of [[Playing a Card]].
Paying the cost generates a [[Delayed Replacement]] effect. Even if the unit loses the **Accelerate** [[Keyword]] during the [[Finalize|finalization]] process, as long as the [[Cost|cost]] was paid, that unit will still enter [[Ready|ready]].

**Accelerate** has no function while on [[The Board]].

*Multiple instances of **Accelerate** are redundant.*

**Accelerate**, and whether or not a [[Unit|unit]] has **Accelerate**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].

**Accelerate** generates a [[Delayed Replacement]] effect that replaces a [[Unit|unit]] entering [[The Board]] [[Exhaust|exhausted]] with it entering [[Ready|ready]]. It does not enter [[Exhaust|exhausted]] and then become [[Ready|ready]]. **Accelerate** **will not** interact with, or trigger, [[Ability|abilities]] that are affected by *[[Unit|units]] becoming [[Ready|ready]]*.
