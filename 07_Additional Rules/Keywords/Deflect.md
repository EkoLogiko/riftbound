**Deflect** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is normally present on [[Permanent|Permanents]].

It is formatted as **"Deflect [X]"**.
- The *X* is referenced in the functional text of the [[Ability|ability]].
- The *X* is referred to as the *Deflect Value*.
- If *X* is omitted, it is presumed to be 1.

It is functionally short for **"Spells and abilities an opponent controls that [[Target|target]] [me/this] cost an amount of [[Power]] equal to [Deflect Value] more to play as an [[Additional Cost]] for each time they choose [me/this]."** The [[Power]] used to pay this cost may always be of any [[Domain]].

>*Example*: A Fury spell targets an Order unit with Deflect. The Power used to pay the Deflect cost can be any Domain; it does not need to match the Domain of the spell or the target.

It is an effect that imposes a *Mandatory* [[Additional Cost]] on [[Spell|Spells]] and [[Ability|Abilities]] that choose the [[Game Object]] that has this [[Ability|ability]].

If a [[Game Object]] has **Deflect**, or has been granted **Deflect**, and is granted **Deflect** by an additional source, the *Deflect Value* of all granted **Deflect** [[Keyword|Keywords]] is **summed**.

**Deflect**, and whether or not a [[Game Object]] has **Deflect**, is a characteristic of the [[Permanent]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
