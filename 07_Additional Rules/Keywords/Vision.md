**Vision** is a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Permanent|Permanents]].

It is functionally short for **"When this is played, [[Predict]].”**
The [[Trigger Condition]] is the permanent entering [[The Board]].

Multiple instances of [[Vision]] **trigger separately**. The player may choose to [[Recycle]] or not [[Recycle]] for each instance of **Vision** separately. If the player does not [[Recycle]] the top card and nothing else happens in between the triggers resolving, each instance of **Vision** will see the same card.

**Vision**, and whether or not a [[Permanent|permanent]] has **Vision**, is a characteristic of the [[Permanent|permanent]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
