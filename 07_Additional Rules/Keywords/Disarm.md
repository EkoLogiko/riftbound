**Disarm** is a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]], present on Units.

**Disarm** is an [[Attack Triggers|Attack Trigger]] and is formatted as “*Disarm X*.”
- The X is referenced in the functional text of the ability.
- The X is referred to as the **Disarm Value**.
- If X is omitted, it is presumed to be 1.
- Disarm is functionally short for *“When I attack, give an enemy unit here -X [M] this turn.”*

Regardless of how many instances of the **Disarm** keyword a unit has, it will only trigger once using the total **Disarm Value**.

If a [[Unit]] has **Disarm**, or has been granted **Disarm**, and is granted **Disarm** by an additional source, the **Disarm Value** of all granted **Disarm** keywords is summed.

**Disarm**, and whether or not a unit has **Disarm**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
