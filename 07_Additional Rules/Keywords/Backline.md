**Backline** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]], present on [[Unit|Units]].

It is functionally short for **“I must be assigned [[Lethal Damage]] after any other [[Unit]] with the same [[Control|Controller]] as me that does not have [Backline] during the [[Combat Damage Step]].”**

It alters how players can elect to [[Damage Assignment|assign combat damage]] during [[Combat]].

Players must still assign [[Lethal Damage]] to a [[Unit]] before moving to the next when assigning their [[Damage]].

If more than one unit with **Backline** is present with the same controller in [[Combat]], [[Damage]] may be assigned to any of them. [[Unit|Units]] with **Backline** are invalid assignments until all [[Unit|units]] without **Backline** have [[Lethal Damage]] assigned to them.

Multiple Instances of **Backline** are **redundant**.

**Backline**, and whether or not a unit has **Backline**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
