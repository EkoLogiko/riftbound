**Assault** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]], present on [[Unit|Units]].

**Assault** is formatted as **"Assault [X]"**.
- The *X* is referenced in the functional text of the [[Ability|ability]].
- The *X* is referred to as the *Assault Value*.
- If *X* is omitted, it is presumed to be 1.

It is functionally short for **"While I am an [[Attacker]], I have +X [[Might|[M]]]."** **Assault** remains in effect as long as the [[Unit]] maintains the [[Attacker]] designation.

If a [[Unit]] has **Assault** or has been granted **Assault** and is granted **Assault** by an additional source, the *Assault Value* of all granted **Assault** [[Keyword|Keywords]] is **summed**.

>*Example*: Petty Officer has Assault. It is chosen as the target of Cleave, which says **"Give a unit [Assault 3] this turn."** After Cleave resolves, Petty Officer has Assault 4 this turn.

**Assault**, and whether or not a unit has **Assault**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
