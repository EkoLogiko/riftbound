**Empower** is an [[Activated Abilities|Activated Ability]] [[Keyword]], normally present on [[Permanent|Permanents]] and [[Champion Legend|Legends]].

**Empower** has a [[Cost|cost]] to activate and [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowers]] the source of the [[Ability|ability]] when the cost is paid.

The source [[Game Object]] is not a [[Target]] of the **Empower** ability.

**Empower** is formatted as **“Empower [ [[Cost]] ]”**.

**Empower** is functionally short for **“[Cost]: [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empower]] this. Play only if not [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]].”**

**Empower** [[Cost|costs]] may include both resource costs and non-resource costs.

**Empower** [[Ability|abilities]] may also include text that alters the **Empower** cost. Such text is taken into account when determining a card’s Empower [[Cost|cost]] for any reason.
**Empower** [[Ability|abilities]] may include text that alters the timing of the **Empower** [[Ability|ability]].

When the [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empower]] action completes from this [[Keyword]], the source [[Permanent|permanent]] or [[Champion Legend|legend]] becomes [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]]. This is an event other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]]and [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] can reference.

Multiple instances of **Empower** are equivalent to multiple [[Activated Abilities|activated abilities]] and can each be [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activated]] separately by paying the corresponding costs.

**Empower** and whether a [[Permanent|permanent]] or [[Champion Legend|legend]] has **Empower** are a characteristic of those [[Game Object|Game Objects]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
