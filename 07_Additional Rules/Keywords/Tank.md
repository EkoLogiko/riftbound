**Tank** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Unit|Units]].

It is functionally short for "I must be assigned [[Lethal Damage]] before any other unit with the same controller as me that does not have **[Tank]** during the [[Combat Damage Step]]."

It alters how players can elect to [[Damage Assignment|assign combat damage]] during [[Combat]].
Players must still assign [[Lethal Damage]] to a [[Unit]] before moving to the next when assigning their [[Damage]].

If **more than one unit with Tank** is present with the same controller in [[Combat]], [[Damage]] may be assigned to any of them. [[Unit|Units]] without **Tank** are invalid assignments until all units with **Tank** have [[Lethal Damage]] assigned to them.

Multiple instances of **Tank** are **redundant**.

**Tank**, and whether or not a unit has **Tank**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
