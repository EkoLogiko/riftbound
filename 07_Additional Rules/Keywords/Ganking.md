Ganking is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]], present on [[Unit|Units]].

It is functionally short for **"I may [[Move]] to a [[Battlefield]] from another [[Battlefield]] with a [[Standard Move]]."**

It is a [[Passive Ability]] that adds permissions to the [[Unit]]'s [[Standard Move]]. It does not restrict or remove options from the [[Unit]]'s [[Standard Move]]. 

It **does not** have an activation [[Cost]].

It does not give additional [[Ability|abilities]] or activations of [[Move|Movement]], only new options for the [[Standard Move]].

Multiple instances of **Ganking** are **redundant**.

**Ganking**, and whether or not a unit has **Ganking**, is a characteristic of the Unit [[Unit]] may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
