**Deploy** is a [[Passive Ability]] and a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]], present on [[Gear]], which is short for:
- *“This may be played or created to any battlefield,”*
- *“Play or create this only to a battlefield,”*
- *“If this is not a unit, it is not recalled in cleanups,”*
- *“When an opponent holds here, kill this.”.*

**Deploy** grants permission for [[Gear]] to be played to any [[Battlefield]], including enemy occupied and open battlefields.

[[Gear]] with **Deploy** can be [[Move|Moved]] by appropriate effects.

Non-Unit [[Gear]] with **Deploy** are not [[Recall|Recalled]] from [[Battlefield|Battlefields]] in step 5 of a [[Cleanup]].

Multiple instances of **Deploy** are redundant.

**Deploy**, and whether or not a card has **Deploy**, is a characteristic of the card and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
