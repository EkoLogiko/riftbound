**Ambush** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is normally present on [[Unit|Units]].

It is functionally short for **"I may be played to a [[Battlefield]] where you [[Control|control]] [[Unit|Units]]"** and **“I have [ [[Reaction]] ] as long as I’m being played to a [[Battlefield]] where you control [[Unit|Units]].”**

It is a [[Passive Ability]] that adds options to locations that are valid for a [[Unit]] to be played to during the step 2: **Make Relevant Choices** step of [[Playing a Card]].

**Ambush** can also appear as a verb on a card. In such a case, the verb is taken to mean **“play with the permissions of the Ambush [[Keyword]].”**

>*Example*: Rengar, Trophy Hunter reads in part **“I can [Ambush] to a battlefield where there are enemy units, even if you don't have units there.”** This ability expands the normal permissions of the Ambush keyword to include battlefields where there are enemy units.
>*Example*: A card reads in part **“Opponents can only [Ambush] at any time they could play cards with [Action].”** This ability introduces a restriction to when opponents can play cards with Ambush.

Multiple instances of **Ambush** are **redundant**.

If there are no [[Unit|units]] at the [[Location]] chosen before [[Finalize|finalization]] completes for any reason, **then it is no longer a valid location** by **Ambush**’s reasoning and cannot be played there. Other effects and permissions may still enable this [[Unit]] to be able to be played to the selected location, but **Ambush**’s permission will not be valid.

**Ambush**, and whether or not a [[Unit]] has **Ambush**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
