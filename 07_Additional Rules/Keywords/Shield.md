**Shield** is a [[Passive Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Unit|Units]].

**Shield** is formatted as **"Shield [X]"**.
- The *X* is referenced in the functional text of the [[Ability|ability]].
- The *X* is referred to as the *Shield Value*.
- If *X* is omitted, it is presumed to be 1.

It is functionally short for **"While I am a [[Defender]], I have +X [[Might|M]]."**

Being a [[Defender]] means the [[Unit]] has gained the [[Defender]] designation during [[Combat]]. **Shield** remains in effect as long as the [[Unit]] maintains the [[Defender]] designation.

If a [[Unit]] has **Shield**, or has been granted **Shield**, and is granted **Shield** by an additional source, the *Shield Value* of all granted **Shield** [[Keyword|Keywords]] is **summed**.

**Shield**, and whether or not a unit has **Shield**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
