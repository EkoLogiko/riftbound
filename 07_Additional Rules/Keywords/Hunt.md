**Hunt** is a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Unit|Units]].

**Hunt** is both a [[Conquer]] and a [[Hold]] effect.

**Hunt** is formatted as **“Hunt X”**.

**Hunt** is functionally short for: **“When I [[Conquer]] or [[Hold]], my [[Control|controller]] gains X [[XP]].”**
- If *X* is omitted, it is presumed to be 1.
- *X* is referred to as the *Hunt Value*.

If a [[Unit]] has **Hunt**, or has been granted **Hunt**, and is granted **Hunt** by an additional source, the *Hunt Value* of all granted **Hunt** [[Keyword|Keywords]] is **summed**.

**Hunt**, and whether or not a [[Unit|unit]] has **Hunt**, is a characteristic of the [[Unit]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
