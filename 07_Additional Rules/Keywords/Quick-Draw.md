**Quick-Draw** is a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is also a *Permissive* [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Gear]] with [[Equip]] [[Ability|abilities]].

Cards with **Quick-Draw** have [[Reaction]] inherently. **Quick-Draw** allows cards to be played and [[Attach|Attached]] using [[Reaction]] timing.

**Quick-Draw** is functionally short for “[ [[Reaction]] ]” and **“When you play this, [[Attach]] it to a [[Unit]] you control.”**

Multiple instances of **Quick-Draw** *do not trigger separately* and have no effect beyond the first.

**Quick-Draw**, and whether or not a [[Gear]] has **Quick-Draw**, is a characteristic of the [[Gear]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
