**Empowered** is a [[Dependent Keyword]].

It is formatted as **“[Empowered] [>] [Text].”**

Starting from the [[Keyword]] to the end of the clause, the entire statement is the **Empowered** [[Ability]].

It is functionally short for **“While I have the [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]] status, this card gains ‘[Text]’.”**
The [Text] here is the [[Dependent Ability]].

As long as the [[Game Object]] has the [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]] status, then the [[Dependent Ability]] will be [[Active]].
If the [[Dependent Ability]] is a [[Triggered Ability]] whose condition is **“When I become Empowered,”** or a permutation thereof, it will be [[Active]] and trigger when its source becomes [[06_Playing the Game/Game Actions/Limited Actions/Empower|Empowered]].

[[Game Object|Game Objects]] with **Empower** abilities may have or gain permissions to be
**Empowered** multiple times. Such Game Objects ignore the normal “Play only if
not Empowered.” restriction on Empower abilities.

**Empowered** and whether or not a card has an **Empowered** [[Ability]] is a characteristic of the card that may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
