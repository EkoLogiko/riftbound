**Equip** is an [[Activated Abilities|Activated Ability]] [[Keyword]]. **Equip** is present on [[Gear]] with the [[Tag]] [[Equipment]].

**Equip** has a [[Cost|cost]] to [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activate]] and [[Attach|Attaches]] the card with **Equip** to a chosen [[Unit]] when the cost is paid. **Equip**’s choice is a [[Target]]. The chosen [[Unit]] will become the [[Top-Most Card]] for the [[Attach]] action.

**Equip** is formatted as **“Equip [Cost]”**.

If paying [[Cost|costs]] or making choices for this [[Ability|ability]] causes [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] to trigger, they will be placed on [[The Chain]] above this [[Ability|ability]] in a [[Pending Chain Item|Pending]] state.

**Equip** is functionally short for **“[Cost]: [[Attach]] this [[Gear]] to a [[Unit]] you control.”**

**Equip** [[Cost|costs]] may include both resource costs and non-resource costs.

**Equip** [[Ability|abilities]] may also include text that alters the **Equip** [[Cost|cost]]. Such text is taken into account when determining a card’s **Equip** [[Cost|cost]] when paying for the [[Ability|ability]].
**Equip** [[Ability|abilities]] may include text that alters the timing or [[Target|targeting]] of the **Equip** [[Ability|ability]].

When the [[Attach]] action completes from this [[Keyword]], the [[Unit]] that was chosen is considered to have been **Equipped** by the [[Gear]] with this ability. This is an event other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] and [[Triggered Ability|Triggered Abilities]] can reference.

**Equipped** is the state of a [[Top-Most Card]] being [[Attach|Attached]] by one or more cards that are [[Equipment]]. The state of being **Equipped** is synchronous with that of the [[Attached]] state of the [[Equipment]].
A [[Top-Most Card]] is **Equipped** as long as one or more of its [[Attached]] cards are [[Equipment]]. The state of being **Equipped** corresponds to a [[Top-Most Card]] having a card with **Equip** that is [[Attached]] to it.

Multiple instances of **Equip** are equivalent to multiple [[Activated Abilities]] and can each be [[Playing or Activating Abilities|activated]] separately by paying the corresponding [[Cost|costs]].

**Equip**, and whether or not a [[Gear]] has **Equip**, is a characteristic of the [[Gear]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]]. Whether or not a [[Gear]] has **Equip** may be referenced even if the [[Rules Text]] of the [[Gear]] is [[Inactive]].
