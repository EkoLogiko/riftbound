Temporary is a [[Triggered Ability]] [[Keyword]]. It is present on [[Permanent|Permanents]].

It is functionally short for **"At the start of this [[Permanent|permanent]]'s controller's [[Beginning Phase]], before [[Scoring|scoring]], [[Kill|kill]] this."**
The [[Trigger Condition]] is the controller of the [[Permanent|permanent]]'s [[Beginning Phase]] starting.

Multiple instances of **Temporary** are **redundant**. Regardless of how many instances there are, the [[Ability|ability]] **will only trigger once**.

**Temporary**, and whether or not a [[Permanent|permanent]] has **Temporary**, is a characteristic of the [[Permanent|permanent]] and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]].
