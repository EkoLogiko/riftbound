**Hidden** is a [[Keyword]] that acts as a prerequisite to perform the [[Hide]] [[Discretionary Action]]. It is present on [[Spell|Spells]], [[Unit|Units]], and [[Gear]].

It is functionally short for **"While this card is in your [[Hand]] or in your [[Champion Zone]] on your turn during an [[Open State]], you may pay [A] to hide this facedown at a [[Battlefield]] you [[Control|control]] that doesn't already have a facedown card hidden there for as long as you [[Control|control]] that [[Battlefield]]. Beginning on the next turn, this gains [[Reaction]] and you may play this, ignoring its [[Base Cost]]."**

It allows the player to take the [[Discretionary Action]] [[Hide]]. Notice that [[Hide]] **is not a subset of [[Play]]**.

**Hiding** a card does not open [[The Chain]].
[[Play|Playing]] a card from facedown (or "from **Hidden**") does open [[The Chain]].

Some choices made while [[Playing a Card]] from **Hidden** are restricted to the [[Battlefield]] where it was hidden. A card cannot be played from **Hidden** if it is a [[Spell]] with no valid [[Target|targets]] under these restrictions.
- A hidden [[Permanent]] must be played to that [[Battlefield]]. This includes **hidden** [[Gear]], and overrides the normal restriction that [[Gear]] have in only being allowed to be played to [[Base]].
- If a hidden [[Spell]] or a [[Play Effects|Play Effect]] of a hidden [[Permanent]] chooses any [[Target|Targets]], those [[Target|Targets]] must be chosen from among options at that [[Battlefield]], unless the [[Ability|ability]] explicitly restricts targeting in a way that makes this impossible. Each [[Target]] is treated separately and individually when processing this rule.
- If a hidden [[Spell]] or a [[Play Effects|Play Effect]] of a hidden [[Permanent]] causes you to play a [[Unit]], you must choose to play that [[Unit]] at that [[Battlefield]].

>*Example*: Blastcone Fae is a unit with Hidden and **“When you play me, give a unit -2 [M] this turn, to a minimum of 1 [M].”** Because this is a play effect, its target must be chosen from among units at the same battlefield if Blastcone Fae was played from Hidden.
>*Example*: Tideturner is a unit with Hidden and **“When you play me, you may choose a unit you control at another location. Move me to its location and it to my original location.”** Because its play effect has a targeting restriction that can never be fulfilled by a unit at its battlefield, its target may be chosen freely from among the available options.
>*Example*: Smoke and Mirrors is a spell that reads in part **“Choose a unit you control and another unit you control at a different location.”** If Smoke and Mirrors is played from hidden, the first unit chosen can be chosen at the battlefield Smoke and Mirrors was played from, so it must be. The second unit chosen explicitly restricts targeting in a way that makes this impossible, so it can be chosen from any location.

Instead of being **hidden**, a card with **Hidden** may be played for its [[Cost|cost]] as normal, at its normal timing with no restrictions on [[Target|targeting]].

Multiple instances of **Hidden** are **redundant**.

**Hidden**, and whether or not a card has **Hidden**, is a characteristic of the card and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect]]. This is independent of the state of being facedown.

A card that is **Hidden** gains [[Reaction]] while facedown or played from facedown, and may be played any time a card with [[Reaction]] may be played as a result. The property is granted to the card in its facedown state, and is not publicly known.
