**XP** is a resource that is accrued, spent, or otherwise modified by Players through the course of play. The amount of **XP** that a player has should be marked clearly. b is a value that can be tracked by counters, dice, cards, or any method that clearly displays the specific value of **XP** that is currently on a Player.

The amount of **XP** a Player has is [[Public Information]].

XP can be **Gained** and **Spent**:
- **Gain** --> Increase the value of **XP** marked on the Player gaining it.
- **Spend** --> Reduce the value of XP marked on the Player spending it.

XP **is not a [[Game Object]]**. Therefore, it cannot be [[Target|targeted]], [[Ready|readied]], or [[Exhaust|exhausted]].

There is no limit to an amount of XP a player can accrue.
# Multiplayer
**XP** **is not shared** between Allies in a [[Mode of Play|Game Mode]] with Teammates.
