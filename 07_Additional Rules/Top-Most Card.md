A **Top-Most Card** is a card that has one or more cards linked to it through the process of [[Attach|Attaching]].
# On Cards Attached
The Top-Most card has all [[Effect Text]] of all cards [[Attached]] to it appended to its [[Rules Text]].
The Top-Most Card has its [[Might]] modulated by the [[Might Bonus]] of all cards [[Attached]] to it.

A Top-Most Card and all cards [[Attached]] to it **are at the same location**. When the Top-Most Card changes locations, all [[Attached]] cards **change locations with it.**

The [[Exhaust|Exhausted]] and [[Ready]] state of the Top-Most Card does not affect nor change the status of the [[Attached]] cards and vice versa. **This is true of all statuses aside from location, Attached, and Top-Most.**

>*Example*: If the top-most card becomes stunned, it does not affect the state of any attached cards.
>*Example*: If an attached card becomes empowered, it does not affect the state of its top-most card.
# On Cards Detached
If the Top-Most Card no longer has any cards [[Attached]] to it after [[Detach|Detaching]], it is no longer a Top-Most Card.

The Top-Most Card ceases to have the [[Effect Text]] of the card being [[Detach|Detached]] appended to its [[Rules Text]].
The Top-Most Card ceases to have its [[Might]] modulated by the [[Might Bonus]] of the card being Detached.

When a Top-Most Card changes zones from [[The Board]] to a [[Non-Board Zone]], all Attached cards **Detach** from it, **remaining in their current zones**.
