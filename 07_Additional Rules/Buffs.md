**Buffs** are [[Counters]] placed on [[Unit|Units]]. **Buffs** can be tracked with a buff reminder card from a Riftbound booster pack or with any spare object in your surroundings.

Buffs can be *added* or *spent*.
- **Add** --> To [[Buff]] a [[Unit]], a player chooses a [[Unit]] and then places a buff on it. That [[Unit]] is [[Buff|Buffed]] for as long as the buff remains on it.
- **Spend** --> Spending a **Buff** removes a single **Buff** [[Counters|Counter]] from a [[Unit]].

A buff cannot be spent from a Unit that does not have a buff. A player can only spend buffs on units they control.

**There can only be one Buff on a Unit at a time.** If a Buff is added, or instructed to be added, on a Unit that already has a Buff, it is not placed instead.

Each **Buff** individually contributes +1 [[Might]] to a Unit.

**Buffs** are [[Game Object|Game Objects]] and may be referenced, counted, or affected by other effects as specified. 
Buffs are [[Counters]], and thus are not [[Target|targeted]] by spells and abilities.

I**f a Unit leaves play, remove all Buffs from it.** A [[Champion]] do not retain Buffs in the [[Champion Zone]], even if they return there somehow.
